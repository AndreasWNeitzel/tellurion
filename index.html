I am a fourth-year PhD candidate at the Instituto de Astrofísica e Ciências do Espaço (IA/CAUP) and the University of Porto, working at the intersection of asteroseismology, Galactic archaeology, and machine learning. My research uses stellar oscillations to reconstruct the formation history of the Milky Way.

This website is a personal project in science communication: 342+ physics simulations spanning the undergraduate and graduate physics curriculum, built from scratch as a public educational resource.
