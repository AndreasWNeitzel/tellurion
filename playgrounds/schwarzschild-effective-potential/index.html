Schwarzschild effective potential and the ISCO

What you are seeing: the effective radial potential $V_{\text{eff}}(r)$ for geodesics in Schwarzschild spacetime. Massive particle: $V_{\text{eff}}(r) = \frac{1}{2}(1 - 2M/r)(1 + L^2/r^2) - \frac{1}{2}$. Photon: $V_{\text{eff}}(r) = \frac{1}{2}(L^2/r^2)(1 - 2M/r)$. A radial geodesic obeys $(dr/d\tau)^2 = E^2 - 1 - 2 V_{\text{eff}}$ (massive), so $V_{\text{eff}}$ acts like a one-dimensional barrier or well.

Two special radii: the photon sphere $r_\gamma = 3M$ is the unstable circular orbit for light (peak of the photon potential); the ISCO is at $r_\text{ISCO} = 6M$, the smallest stable circular orbit for massive matter. Below ISCO ($L < 2\sqrt{3} M$) no stable circular orbit exists; matter spirals inward.
