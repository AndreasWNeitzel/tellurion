Henon strange attractor

The Henon 1976 map x' = 1 - a x^2 + y, y' = b x. At the canonical a = 1.4, b = 0.3 the iterates settle onto a strange attractor with box-count dimension ~ 1.26 and max-Lyapunov ~ 0.4192. Tune a and b to visit the period-doubling cascade and the Henon-Smale horseshoe regime.
