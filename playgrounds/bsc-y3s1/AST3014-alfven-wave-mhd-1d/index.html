Alfvén wave in 1D plasma

What you are seeing: a transverse perturbation of the magnetic field propagates along the background $B_0\hat x$ at the Alfvén speed $v_A = B_0 / \sqrt{\mu_0 \rho}$. Field-line tension is the restoring force. The orange curve is $b_y(x, t)$; the cyan curve is $v_y(x, t)$. They are in (anti-)phase: $v_y = \mp b_y / \sqrt{\mu_0 \rho}$. Source: Goedbloed-Poedts Ch. 5 (`goedbloed-plasma`).
