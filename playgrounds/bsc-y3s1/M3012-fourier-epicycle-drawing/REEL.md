# Reel script: Fourier Epicycle Drawing

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A chain of rotating circles, sorted largest to smallest, redraws any closed path through the discrete Fourier transform.
Caption: A chain of rotating circles, sorted large…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: For N complex sample points, compute C k via DFT and reconstruct z(t) = sum k C k exp(2 pi i k t / N).
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: With M epicycles the reconstruction error decreases monotonically; full N/2 reproduces the path within float precision.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)

VO: Push it to an extreme and see where the physics breaks down.
Caption: your turn
