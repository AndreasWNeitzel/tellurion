Fabry-Perot etalon: Airy transmission

What you are seeing: the transmission of a Fabry-Perot etalon (two parallel mirrors of reflectance $R$ at spacing $L$) as a function of round-trip phase $\phi = 4 \pi n L \cos\theta / \lambda$. The Airy distribution $T(\phi) = 1 / (1 + F \sin^2(\phi/2))$ has sharp transmission peaks at $\phi = 2 \pi m$ (resonance) with width controlled by the coefficient of finesse $F = 4R/(1-R)^2$.

The finesse $F_* = \pi \sqrt{R} / (1 - R)$ measures how many FWHM fit inside a free spectral range. A laser cavity ($R \approx 0.99$) has $F_* \approx 313$, giving extremely narrow peaks; a low-finesse etalon ($R = 0.5$) has $F_* \approx 4.4$ and broad peaks.
