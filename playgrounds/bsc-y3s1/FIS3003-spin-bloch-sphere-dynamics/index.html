Spin on the Bloch Sphere

Add a circularly polarized RF field of amplitude B1B_1B1​ at frequency ωrf\omega_{\mathrm{rf}}ωrf​ and the vector also nutates: on resonance (ωrf=ω0\omega_{\mathrm{rf}}=\omega_0ωrf​=ω0​) it sweeps pole to pole, so a π\piπ-pulse inverts ∣0⟩→∣1⟩|0\rangle\to|1\rangle∣0⟩→∣1⟩ and a π/2\pi/2π/2-pulse builds an equal superposition.
