Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions on [0, pi]

What you are seeing: the orthonormal eigenfunctions $\phi_n(x) = \sqrt{2/\pi} \sin(n x)$ of $-y'' = \lambda y$ on $[0, \pi]$ with $y(0) = y(\pi) = 0$. Eigenvalues are $\lambda_n = n^2$. The right panel plots a target function $f(x) = x (\pi - x)$ and its truncated Fourier-Sturm-Liouville reconstruction $\sum_{n=1}^{N} c_n \phi_n(x)$ where $c_n = \langle \phi_n, f\rangle$.

As you increase $N$, the reconstruction converges to $f$. The expansion uses only odd $n$ because $f$ is symmetric about $\pi/2$ while $\phi_n$ for even $n$ is antisymmetric there.
