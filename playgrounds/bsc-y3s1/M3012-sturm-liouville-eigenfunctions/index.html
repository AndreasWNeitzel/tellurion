Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions on [0, pi]

What you are seeing: the Sturm-Liouville modes of $-y'' = \lambda y$ on $[0, \pi]$ with $y(0) = y(\pi) = 0$ are the normal modes of a clamped string. The top panel is a vibrating string evolving as $y(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} c_n \phi_n(x) \cos(\omega_n t)$ with $\omega_n = \sqrt{\lambda_n} = n$, so the eigenvalue spectrum $\lambda_n = n^2$ becomes visible motion. The lower lanes show each normal mode oscillating at its own rate (mode $n$ is $n$ times faster).

Click the string to re-pluck it (a triangular initial condition at the cursor) and watch the modal mix change. The $N$ slider sets how many eigenfunctions reconstruct the profile, with the maximum reconstruction error read out live.
