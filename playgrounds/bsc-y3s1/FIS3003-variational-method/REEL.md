# Reel script: The Variational Method

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Guess a wavefunction, compute its energy, and you have an upper bound on the ground state you can never beat.
Caption: Guess a wavefunction, compute its energy,…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Most quantum systems cannot be solved exactly, but there is a remarkably simple way to bound the ground-state energy from above: guess a wavefunction.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The variational theorem guarantees that for any normalized trial state, the expectation $\langle H\rangle$ is greater than or equal to the true ground-state energy $E_0$, with equality only if your guess happens to be the exact ground state. So you build a trial wavefunction with an adjustable knob, compute the energy, and slide the knob to push $\langle H\rangle$ as low as it will go; the minimum is your best estimate, and you know it is an honest upper bound.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Sweep the width $a$: the energy $\langle H\rangle$ slides along its curve but never drops below $E_0$.
VO: Jump to the best $a^\ast$: the minimum sits at $-0.424$ Ha, the closest a single Gaussian can get.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: That floor is the whole content of the variational principle.
