Neutron Star LEGEND

What you are seeing: a rotating 1.4 M_sun neutron star rendered through five modes (Overview, Lighthouse, Magnetar, Structure, Spindown). The same NS object is shared across modes so you build one mental model of the second-most-extreme object in the universe. Drag the canvas to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom. Source: Shapiro and Teukolsky, Black Holes, White Dwarfs and Neutron Stars, 1983, Ch. 9 (shapiro-teukolsky-bh-wd-ns).
