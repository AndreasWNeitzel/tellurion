Black Hole LEGEND

What you are seeing: a Schwarzschild and Kerr black hole rendered through ten modes (Overview, Photons, Lensing, Shadow, Frame drag, Spacetime, Ringdown, Hawking radiation, TDE flare, Tidal). The lensed accretion disk is a backward ray-march in the Schwarzschild metric with first-order Kerr correction. Drag the canvas to orbit; scroll to zoom. Reference: Misner, Thorne, Wheeler, Gravitation, Ch. 25 (mtw-gravitation); Bardeen, Press, Teukolsky 1972; Luminet 1979.
