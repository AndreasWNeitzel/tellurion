Single, double, and multi-slit diffraction

What you are seeing: the Fraunhofer diffraction pattern for $N$ identical slits of width $a$ and center-to-center separation $d$: $I(\theta) = I_0 \, \text{sinc}^2(\pi a \sin\theta / \lambda) \, [\sin(N \pi d \sin\theta / \lambda) / \sin(\pi d \sin\theta / \lambda)]^2$. The first factor is the single-slit envelope; the second is the array factor that produces sharp principal maxima at $d \sin\theta = m \lambda$.

The faint orange curve is the single-slit envelope; the cyan curve is the full $N$-slit pattern. As $N$ grows, principal maxima sharpen as $1/N$ and their peak intensity grows as $N^2$. The bottom strip is the intensity rendered as a screen image.
