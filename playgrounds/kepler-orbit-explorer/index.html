Kepler orbit explorer

A test particle orbits a fixed central mass under inverse-square gravity in 2D (GM = 1, geometric units). The velocity-Verlet integrator from shared/js/engine/symplectic.js conserves energy and angular momentum to high precision across thousands of periods. Adjust the semi-major axis a and eccentricity e to set the orbit. The trail traces the past 1500 samples of the particle's position; the readouts on the right report the conserved quantities and the live energy drift.
