Michelson interferometer fringe counter

What you are seeing: a Michelson interferometer with one movable mirror. Moving the mirror by $\lambda/2$ produces one full fringe (the optical path difference changes by $\lambda$ since the light travels the arm twice). The ring pattern is the divergent-source case; the count tracks how many fringes pass the center as the mirror moves. Source: Hecht Optics Ch. 9.4 (`hecht2017`).
