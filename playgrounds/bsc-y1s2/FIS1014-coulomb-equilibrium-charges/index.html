Coulomb equilibrium of charges

What you are seeing: four fixed charges (the squares) generate a 2D electrostatic potential. A test charge (the dot) you drag is repelled by like charges and attracted by opposite charges. Equilibria are the points where the local force vanishes; their stability is governed by $\nabla^2 V = 0$ (Earnshaw: equilibria are saddles, never stable). Source: Griffiths, Introduction to Electrodynamics, Ch. 2.
