# Reel script: Poynting Vector: a Plane EM Wave in 3D

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: E and B are not just out of phase by ninety degrees in time, they live in perpendicular planes, and their cross product is exactly where the energy goes.
Caption: E and B are not just out of phase by nine…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A light wave is a braid of two fields: an electric field and a magnetic field, oscillating at right angles to each other and to the direction the wave travels.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Their cross product, the Poynting vector S = E x B, points the way the energy flows and measures how much. For a wave travelling forward, E and B rise and fall together, so S is always positive: energy is carried steadily in one direction, never backward, even though the fields themselves keep flipping sign.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Watch the linear wave: E and B peak together, and the gold energy-flow arrows stay forward, never reversing.
VO: Read the lower plot: the Poynting curve never dips below zero, and its dashed average sits at half the peak, the intensity.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The scene shows the E and B fields propagating with the energy-flow arrows; the diagnostic plots the three quantities and the time-averaged flow.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
