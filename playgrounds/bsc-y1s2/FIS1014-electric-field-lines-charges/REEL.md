# Reel script: Electric Field Lines from Point Charges

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Electric field lines make the invisible field visible: each line is everywhere tangent to E, and where the lines crowd together the field is strong.
Caption: Electric field lines make the invisible f…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Every charge fills the space around it with an electric field, and the cleanest way to picture that field is to draw its lines: curves that start on positive charges and end on negative ones, always pointing the way a tiny positive test charge would be pushed.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Where the lines crowd together the field is strong; where they spread apart it is weak. That is not a drawing convention, it is Gauss's law: the number of lines through any patch is proportional to the field strength there.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Drag a charge with the mouse and watch every field line reshape in real time.
VO: Switch to two like charges: the lines repel and a dead spot opens between them, a clean zero in the lower plot.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The scene shows the field lines flowing over a map of the field strength; the diagnostic is the field strength along the horizontal axis, where the spikes mark the charges and the dips mark the points where the field vanishes.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
