## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The voltage $V_C(t) = V_0 e^{-t/\tau}$ drops to 37 percent (that is $1/e$) at the time constant $\tau = RC$, the characteristic time of every timing circuit. The current is largest at the very start and dies away with the same exponential, so the resistor dissipates the most power, $P = I^2 R$, at $t = 0$ and cools as the current fades.
