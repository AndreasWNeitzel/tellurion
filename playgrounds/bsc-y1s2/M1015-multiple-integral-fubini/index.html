Multiple integrals: Fubini equality

What you are seeing: a heatmap of $f(x, y) = \sin x \cos y$ on $[0, A] \times [0, B]$. Two integration orders ($dx\,dy$ vs $dy\,dx$) give numerically identical values, illustrating Fubini's theorem. Source: Riley-Hobson Ch. 10 (`riley-hobson`).
