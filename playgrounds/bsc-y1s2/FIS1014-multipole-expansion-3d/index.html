Multipole Expansion: Exact vs Truncated Potential

The exact electrostatic potential of a charge cluster, the multipole expansion $V=K[Q/r+(\mathbf p\cdot\hat{\mathbf r})/r^2+\tfrac12 Q_{ij}\hat r_i\hat r_j/r^3+\dots]$ truncated at a chosen order, and the error between them, shown as three field maps. The error blows up near the charges and collapses far away, and each extra term tightens it. A sweeping probe traces the error against distance.
