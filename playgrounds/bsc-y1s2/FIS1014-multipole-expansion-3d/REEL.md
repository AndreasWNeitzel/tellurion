# Reel script: Multipole Expansion: Exact vs Truncated Potential

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Far away, even a messy charge cluster looks like one number.
Caption: Far away, even a messy charge cluster loo…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Far enough away, any clump of charge stops looking complicated.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Its potential can be written as a sum of simpler pieces: a monopole that falls off as one over distance, a dipole that falls as one over distance squared, a quadrupole as distance cubed, and so on, each weaker and shorter-ranged than the last. This is the multipole expansion, and it is why a distant galaxy is a point mass, a molecule is a dipole, and an antenna is designed term by term.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Drag the probe far out: the truncated potential locks onto the exact one and the error plunges.
VO: Keep only the monopole for the dipole cloud: the error sits near 100%, the net charge is zero so the leading term is useless.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The scene shows the true potential of a small charge cluster with a probe you can move; the diagnostic plots how wrong each truncation is against distance, on a log-log scale where every extra term buys a steeper drop in the error.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
