Brewster angle and the Fresnel reflectance

What you are seeing: light incident on a planar dielectric interface from medium of index n1n_1n1​ onto medium of index n2n_2n2​. The reflectance for sss-polarized light (electric field perpendicular to the plane of incidence) and ppp-polarized light (electric field in the plane) follow the Fresnel formulas. The right panel shows Rs(θi)R_s(\theta_i)Rs​(θi​) and Rp(θi)R_p(\theta_i)Rp​(θi​) over the full range of incidence angles. The animated ray sketch on the left shows incoming, reflected, and transmitted beams.

At Brewster's angle θB=arctan⁡(n2/n1)\theta_B = \arctan(n_2 / n_1)θB​=arctan(n2​/n1​) the ppp-reflectance drops exactly to zero: light at this angle is reflected purely sss-polarized. Polarizing sunglasses exploit this by filtering out sss-polarized glare from horizontal surfaces.
