Interactive Laplace Solver: Draw Your Own Conductors

Drag on the canvas to paint charged conductors; the potential $\varphi$ relaxes toward the solution of $\nabla^2\varphi=0$ by GPU-style successive over-relaxation and the electric-field streamlines $\mathbf E=-\nabla\varphi$ form in about a second. Presets give the parallel-plate capacitor, coaxial cable, dipole and charged sphere; the field always meets each conductor normally.
