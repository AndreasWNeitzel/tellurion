Method of images: charge above a grounded plane

What you are seeing: a real point charge qqq at height bbb above a grounded conducting plane induces a surface charge on the conductor. The field above the plane is identical to that of the real charge plus an image charge −q-q−q at depth bbb below the plane. Drag the real charge; the image charge follows by mirror reflection.
