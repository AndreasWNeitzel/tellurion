Method of images: charge above a grounded plane

What you are seeing: a real point charge $q$ at height $b$ above a grounded conducting plane induces a surface charge on the conductor. The field above the plane is identical to that of the real charge plus an image charge $-q$ at depth $b$ below the plane. Drag the real charge; the image charge follows by mirror reflection. Source: Griffiths E&M Ch. 3.2 (`griffiths-em`).
