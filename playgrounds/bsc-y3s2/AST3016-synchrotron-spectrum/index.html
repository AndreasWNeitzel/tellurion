Synchrotron spectrum

What you are seeing: a single electron radiates a humped spectrum peaked at $\nu_c \propto \gamma^2 B$. A power-law electron distribution $N(\gamma) \propto \gamma^{-p}$ produces a power-law photon spectrum $F_\nu \propto \nu^{-(p-1)/2}$. Source: Rybicki-Lightman Ch. 6 (`rybickilightman1979`).
