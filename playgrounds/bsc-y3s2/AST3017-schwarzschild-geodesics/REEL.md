# Reel script: Schwarzschild Light Bending

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Aim a light ray near a black hole: outside a critical impact parameter it bends and escapes, inside it spirals through the photon sphere and is swallowed.
Caption: Aim a light ray near a black hole: outsid…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A horizontal plane wave of photons enters from the left and meets a non-rotating black hole at the origin.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Geometric units G = c = M = 1. Each photon obeys a null geodesic with conserved energy E and angular momentum L; its fate is fixed by the impact parameter b = L / E.
Caption: the physics, simply
