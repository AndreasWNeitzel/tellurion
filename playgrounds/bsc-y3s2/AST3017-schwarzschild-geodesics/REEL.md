## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The playground fires a plane wave of photons past a non-rotating black hole: rays with b above the critical value 3 sqrt 3 (about 5. 2 M) bend and escape, weakly deflected by about 4M/b far out; rays below it cross the photon sphere at r = 3M and fall through the horizon.
