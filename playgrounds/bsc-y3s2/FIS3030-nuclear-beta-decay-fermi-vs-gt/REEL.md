## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: In nuclear beta decay an electron and an antineutrino share the released energy, so the electron spectrum is continuous.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Two channels contribute: Fermi transitions (electron and neutrino spins antiparallel, no nuclear spin change) and Gamow-Teller transitions (spins parallel, nuclear spin can change by one), each with its own selection rules. The playground shows the spectrum and the Kurie plot, a rescaling that turns the allowed spectrum into a straight line whose intercept is the endpoint energy and a probe of the neutrino mass.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Set the spin change between initial and final state: a Fermi transition needs Delta J = 0 with no parity flip, Gamow-Teller allows Delta J = 0 or 1 (but not 0 to 0).
VO: Toggle the parity change: an allowed decay forbids it, so flipping parity forces the transition to be forbidden, suppressing the rate by orders of magnitude.
