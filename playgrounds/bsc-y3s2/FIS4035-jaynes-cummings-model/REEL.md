## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Each photon-number doublet $\{|e,n\rangle, |g,n+1\rangle\}$ oscillates at the quantum Rabi frequency $\Omega_n = 2 g \sqrt{n+1}$, so for a field with photon distribution $P(n)$ the atomic inversion is the exact closed-form sum $W(t) = \sum_n P(n) \cos(2 g t \sqrt{n+1})$ with $P_e + P_g = 1$ by construction. For a coherent state the Poissonian spread of Rabi frequencies dephases the oscillation: it collapses on $t_c \sim \sqrt{2}/g$ (set by the frequency spread $\sim g$, hence independent of the mean photon number $\bar{n}$) and then, because the frequencies are discrete, rephases into a revival near $t_r = 2\pi\sqrt{\bar{n}}/g$.
