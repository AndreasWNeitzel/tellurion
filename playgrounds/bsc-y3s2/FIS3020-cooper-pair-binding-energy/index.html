Cooper-pair binding energy

What you are seeing: two electrons added to a filled Fermi sea bind into a Cooper pair with energy $E_{bind} = 2\hbar\omega_D e^{-2/N(0)V}$. Exponentially small at weak coupling, the binding is nonperturbative in the attractive interaction strength. Source: Ashcroft-Mermin Ch. 34 (`ashcroft-mermin`).
