Parallel transport on a sphere

What you are seeing: a "Beltrami" spherical triangle on the unit sphere with one vertex at the north pole and the other two on a colatitude α\alphaα circle, separated by longitude β\betaβ. Parallel transport of any vector around the triangle returns it rotated by the enclosed solid angle Ω=(1−cos⁡α)β\Omega = (1 - \cos\alpha) \betaΩ=(1−cosα)β.
