2D Navier-Stokes Vortex Street

Incompressible flow past a bluff body, the Chorin projection of $\partial_t\vec u+(\vec u\cdot\nabla)\vec u=-\nabla p+\nu\nabla^2\vec u$ with $\nabla\cdot\vec u=0$, on a MAC staggered grid (semi-Lagrangian advection, implicit diffusion, an SOR pressure-Poisson projection). The colour is vorticity $\omega=\partial_xv-\partial_yu$. Raise the Reynolds number $Re=UD/\nu$ and the wake goes from glassy creeping flow, through a steady recirculating pair, to the periodic von Karman street whose shedding beat is the Strouhal number, to a broadband 2D wake. The numerics are the gate-tested shared MAC engine; the live readout is the post-projection divergence. Source: Chorin, Math. Comput. 22 (1968) 745 (`chorin1968`); Harlow and Welch, Phys. Fluids 8 (1965) 2182 (`harlow-welch1965`); Stam, SIGGRAPH 99 (1999) 121 (`stam1999`).
