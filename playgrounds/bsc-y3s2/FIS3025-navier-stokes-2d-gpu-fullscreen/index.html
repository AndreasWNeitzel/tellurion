Incompressible Wake and the Projection Method

Chorin's projection method made visible: the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations $\partial_t\vec u+(\vec u\cdot\nabla)\vec u=-\nabla p+\nu\nabla^2\vec u$ with the constraint $\nabla\cdot\vec u=0$, solved on a MAC staggered grid (semi-Lagrangian advection, implicit diffusion, an SOR pressure-Poisson projection, the gate-tested shared engine). Flow past a bluff body, coloured by vorticity $\omega=\partial_xv-\partial_yu$ over the dark field. The headline is the live $\max|\nabla\cdot\vec u|$ readout: the pressure solve drives it small every step, so the flow stays incompressible. Sweep the Reynolds number $Re=UD/\nu$ from a glassy creep, through a steady recirculating wake, to a broader unsteady wake. (At this interactive grid the semi-Lagrangian numerical viscosity holds the effective $Re$ below the von Karman shedding threshold, so the periodic vortex street and its Strouhal number are a documented finer-grid feature, not shown here.) Source: Chorin, Math. Comput. 22 (1968) 745 (`chorin1968`); Harlow and Welch, Phys. Fluids 8 (1965) 2182 (`harlow-welch1965`); Stam, SIGGRAPH 99 (1999) 121 (`stam1999`).
