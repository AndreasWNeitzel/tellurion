# Zeeman to Paschen-Back
Low-$B$ Zeeman: $g_J m_J \mu_B B$. High-$B$ Paschen-Back: $(m_L + 2m_S) \mu_B B$. Source: Griffiths QM Ch. 6.4 (`griffiths-qm`).
