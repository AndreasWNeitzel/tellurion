Zeeman to Paschen-Back crossover

What you are seeing: the 2p levels of hydrogen as a function of magnetic field $B$. At weak fields the splitting is $g_J m_J \mu_B B$ (Zeeman). At strong field $\mathbf L$ and $\mathbf S$ decouple and the splitting becomes $(m_L + 2 m_S) \mu_B B$ (Paschen-Back). Crossover occurs when Zeeman energy equals fine-structure energy. Source: Griffiths QM Ch. 6.4 (`griffiths-qm`).
