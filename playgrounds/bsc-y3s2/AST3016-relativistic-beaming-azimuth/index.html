Relativistic beaming pattern

What you are seeing: a light source that emits equally in all directions in its own rest frame. When the source is moving toward you at high speed, special relativity concentrates the emission into a narrow cone pointing forward (along the direction of motion). This is "relativistic beaming". It is why blazar jets look much brighter when their axis points at us and dim when it does not.
