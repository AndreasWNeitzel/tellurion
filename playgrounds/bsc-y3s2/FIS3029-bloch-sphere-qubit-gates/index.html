Bloch sphere qubit gates

A single qubit lives on the Bloch sphere; pure states are points on the unit 2-sphere. Apply gates (X, Y, Z, H, S, T) or continuous rotations (R_x, R_y, R_z) and watch the state vector trace its path. The trail shows the last few moves; the readout reports the live (theta, phi), the Bloch components (r_x, r_y, r_z), and the unitarity norm of the most recent gate (should be machine zero).
