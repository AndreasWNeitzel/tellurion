# Mandelstam $s$: fixed-target vs collider
$\sqrt s \propto \sqrt{E_{lab}}$ for fixed targets, but $\propto E_{lab}$ for symmetric colliders. Source: Griffiths Particles Ch. 3 (`griffiths-particles`).
