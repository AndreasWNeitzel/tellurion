Gaussian curvature of 2D surfaces

What you are seeing: a torus colored by Gaussian curvature: positive on the outer rim, negative on the inner rim, zero on the top/bottom circles. The sphere has constant $K = 1/R^2 \gt 0$, the hyperbolic plane has constant $K \lt 0$, the cylinder has $K = 0$. Source: Riley-Hobson Ch. 26 (`riley-hobson`).
