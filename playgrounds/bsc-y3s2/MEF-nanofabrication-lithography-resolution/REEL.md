VO: Image a mask through a lens and the pupil throws away every spatial frequency above NA/lambda: features finer than the Rayleigh half-pitch R = k1 lambda / NA blur to flat grey.

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)

Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: A reticle (mask) transmission t(x) is imaged through a lens of numerical aperture NA at wavelength lambda; the pupil passes only spatial frequencies |f| <= NA/lambda, so the aerial intensity is I(x) = |IFFT[ pupil. FFT(t) ]| 2 and the resolvable half-pitch is the Rayleigh limit R = k1 lambda / NA.
Caption: the physics, simply
