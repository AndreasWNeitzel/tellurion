# Reel script: Crystal Structure Explorer

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Spin SC, BCC and FCC cells and read off why their powder X-ray patterns follow 1,2,3.., 2,4,6..
Caption: Spin SC, BCC and FCC cells and read off w…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: The three cubic Bravais lattices shown in a rotating 3D view: the conventional cell with its atoms, a chosen Miller plane, the reciprocal lattice, and the powder X-ray diffraction pattern.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The reciprocal basis satisfies b i. a j = 2 pi delta ij, the cubic interplanar spacing is d = a / sqrt(h 2 + k 2 + l 2), and the geometric structure factor produces systematic absences (body-centred: h+k+l even; face-centred: h,k,l all even or all odd) so the Bragg lines fall in the distinct simple-, body- and face-centred sequences that let X-ray diffraction identify a crystal.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Drag a control and watch the whole picture change, not just a number on the side.
VO: Push it to an extreme and see where the physics breaks down.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The first Brillouin zones are the cube, the rhombic dodecahedron and the truncated octahedron.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
