## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The atom basis sets the geometric structure factor, so some reflections vanish: BCC needs h+k+lh+k+lh+k+l even, FCC needs h,k,lh,k,lh,k,l all even or all odd, which is why their powder lines follow the sequences 2,4,6,8 and 3,4,8,11.
