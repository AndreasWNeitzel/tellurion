The three cubic Bravais lattices, simple (SC), body-centred (BCC) and face-centred (FCC), with their conventional cells, a chosen Miller plane (hkl)(hkl)(hkl) at spacing d=a/h2+k2+l2d=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}d=a/h2+k2+l2​, and the powder X-ray pattern. The atom basis sets the geometric structure factor, so some reflections vanish: BCC needs h+k+lh+k+lh+k+l even, FCC needs h,k,lh,k,lh,k,l all even or all odd, which is why their powder lines follow the sequences 2,4,6,8 and 3,4,8,11. The reciprocal lattice (defined by b⃗i⋅a⃗j=2πδij\vec b_i\cdot\vec a_j=2\pi\delta_{ij}bi​⋅aj​=2πδij​) is FCC for a BCC crystal and BCC for an FCC one, so the first Brillouin zones are the rhombic dodecahedron (12 faces) and the truncated octahedron (14 faces).
