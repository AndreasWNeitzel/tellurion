# Reel script: Landau Levels

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A magnetic field forces electrons into circles and quantizes their energy into a ladder.
Caption: A magnetic field forces electrons into ci…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A charged particle in a magnetic field has no choice but to go in circles.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Classically it traces a cyclotron orbit at the frequency $\omega_c = eB/m$, tighter and faster the stronger the field. Quantize that circular motion and something striking happens to the energy: instead of the smooth continuum of a free particle, only a ladder of discrete values is allowed, the Landau levels $E_n = (n+\tfrac12)\hbar\omega_c$, evenly spaced rungs whose separation grows in direct proportion to $B$.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Raise the field $B$: the cyclotron orbit tightens and speeds up, and the Landau levels spread apart.
VO: Keep raising it: filled levels climb past $E_F$ one by one and empty out, the count of filled levels dropping.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: As the field increases each line climbs and one by one they cross the Fermi energy and empty out, and that periodic depopulation is the microscopic origin of the de Haas-van Alphen and Shubnikov-de Haas oscillations, the tool experimentalists use to map the Fermi surfaces of metals.
