Alpha decay via Gamow tunneling

What you are seeing: the Geiger-Nuttall relationship between alpha-decay half-life and Q-value. The Coulomb barrier at right is the tunnel that the alpha particle must escape; the half-life is exponentially sensitive to $Q_\alpha$ via the Gamow penetration factor. Source: Krane Nuclear Physics Ch. 8 (`krane-nuclear`).
