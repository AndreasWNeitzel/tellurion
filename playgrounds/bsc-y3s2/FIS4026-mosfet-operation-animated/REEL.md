## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: 10-11; Sze and Ng). Below threshold (V GS < V th) the device is off and the drain current is a tiny subthreshold exponential; above threshold the inversion channel forms and, for V DS < V ov = V GS - V th, the device is in the triode region with I D = k n[V ov V DS - V DS 2/2]; at V DS = V ov the channel pinches off at the drain and the device saturates at I D = (k n/2) V ov 2 (1 + lambda V DS).
