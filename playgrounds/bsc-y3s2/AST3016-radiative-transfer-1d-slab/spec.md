# 1D radiative transfer
Slab with constant $S$ and finite $\tau$; closed-form $I(\tau) = I_{in} e^{-\tau} + S(1-e^{-\tau})$. Source: Rybicki-Lightman Ch. 1 (`rybickilightman1979`).
