## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The binding energy is a volume term (a V A) minus a surface term (a S A 2/3), minus Coulomb repulsion, minus a proton-neutron asymmetry term, plus a pairing term. Together they reproduce the binding-energy-per-nucleon curve that peaks near iron, which is why light nuclei release energy by fusion and heavy nuclei by fission.
Caption: the physics, simply
