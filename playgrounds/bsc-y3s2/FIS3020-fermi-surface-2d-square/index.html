Fermi surface on a 2D square lattice

What you are seeing: the Brillouin zone (kx, ky) in [−π,π]2[-\pi, \pi]^2[−π,π]2 for a tight-binding electron on a square lattice with dispersion E(kx,ky)=−2t(cos⁡kx+cos⁡ky)E(k_x, k_y) = -2t (\cos k_x + \cos k_y)E(kx​,ky​)=−2t(coskx​+cosky​). Below half-filling the Fermi surface is a closed loop around the Γ\GammaΓ point; at half-filling it is the perfectly nested square diagonals (van Hove singularity in DOS); above half-filling it shifts to closed loops around the (pi, pi) corners.

Sliding the filling fraction fff from 0 to 1 sweeps through these three regimes. The right panel shows the density of states with the Fermi energy marked; the van Hove peak at E=0E = 0E=0 is the signature of the saddle points at (±π,0)(\pm \pi, 0)(±π,0) and (0,±π)(0, \pm \pi)(0,±π).
