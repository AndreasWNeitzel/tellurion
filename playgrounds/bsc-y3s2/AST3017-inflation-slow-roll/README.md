# Slow-roll inflation: $n_s$ vs $r$

The two observables of slow-roll inflation are the scalar spectral index $n_s$ and the tensor-to-scalar ratio $r$. Four canonical models map to distinct points on the $(n_s, r)$ plane: $\phi^2$, $\phi^4$, natural inflation, and Starobinsky $R^2$. Only Starobinsky comfortably sits inside the Planck 2018 box; the chaotic models are pushed out by the tensor upper limit $r < 0.064$.

Look for the green Planck box: only Starobinsky's trajectory stays inside as $N$ varies. The chaotic $\phi^4$ predicts $r \sim 0.27$ at $N = 60$, well outside any current constraint.

Two controls: e-folds $N$ and model selector.
