Slow-roll inflation: $n_s$ vs $r$

What you are seeing: the predictions of slow-roll inflationary models on the $(n_s, r)$ plane, with the Planck 2018 constraint box overlaid. The spectral index $n_s = 1 - 6\epsilon + 2\eta$ and tensor-to-scalar ratio $r = 16\epsilon$ follow from the inflaton potential $V(\phi)$ via the slow-roll parameters $\epsilon = (V'/V)^2/2$ and $\eta = V''/V$.

Models tracked: $\phi^2$ (chaotic quadratic, excluded), $\phi^4$ (chaotic quartic, far excluded), natural inflation (depending on $f$), and Starobinsky $R^2$ inflation (favored, sits well inside the box). Trajectories sweep through $N = 50$ to $80$ e-folds before the end of inflation.
