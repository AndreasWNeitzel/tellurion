Gravitational-Wave Chirp Sonification

A compact-binary inspiral. Two perspective-shaded spheres orbit on an inclined plane at the barycentric Kepler separation $a \propto f_\mathrm{orb}^{-2/3}$ driven by the post-Newtonian chirp $f(t)$, trailing decaying spiral worldlines while gravitational-wave fronts expand outward. When the separation collapses the bodies coalesce in a flash into a single ringing remnant. Compact strips scroll the strain $h(t)$ (clamped near merger) and the rising frequency; sliders set $m_1, m_2$, distance and inclination. WebAudio plays the chirp shifted to the audible band.
