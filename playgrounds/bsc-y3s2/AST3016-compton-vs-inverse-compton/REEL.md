## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: In ordinary forward Compton scattering a photon strikes a nearly-at-rest electron and loses energy, capped at E'' = E / (1 + 2E/m e c 2) for a head-on bounce. In inverse Compton a relativistic electron of Lorentz factor gamma hits a low-energy photon and boosts it by roughly gamma 2, to E typ ~ (4/3) gamma 2 E in the Thomson limit.
