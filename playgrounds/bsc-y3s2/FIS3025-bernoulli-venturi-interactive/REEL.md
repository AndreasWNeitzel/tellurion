## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Mass conservation fixes the volume flow Q = A(x) v(x) = const, so the fluid must speed up where the pipe narrows; Bernoulli''s relation p + 1/2 rho v 2 = const along a horizontal streamline then forces the static pressure to drop exactly where the flow is fastest, the counter-intuitive heart of the effect. The scene draws the pipe, tracer particles accelerating through the throat, piezometer columns whose heights read the local static pressure (tall in the wide sections, a stub at the throat), and a thin-airfoil lift cartoon driven by the same principle.
