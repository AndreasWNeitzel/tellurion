Friedmann cosmography

What you are seeing: the scale factor $a(t)$ for a flat LCDM universe, built from the Friedmann equation. It runs from the Big Bang through matter-era deceleration, past the acceleration onset, into the $\Lambda$-dominated future (solid past, dashed future, today at $a = 1$). A comoving patch of galaxies expands and redshifts with $a(t)$ as cosmic time sweeps.

Sliders set $\Omega_m$ and $H_0$ ($\Omega_\Lambda$ fixed by flatness), reshaping the whole history: the Einstein-de Sitter limit ($\Omega_m = 1$) never accelerates, while small $\Omega_m$ approaches a de Sitter exponential expansion. Planck 2018 defaults give $t_0 \approx 13.8$ Gyr.
