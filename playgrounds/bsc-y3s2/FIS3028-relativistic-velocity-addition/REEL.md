# Reel script: Relativistic Velocity Addition

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Half light speed plus half light speed is not light speed.
Caption: Half light speed plus half light speed is…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Fire a ball forward at half the speed of light from a ship already moving at half the speed of light, and common sense says the ball moves at the speed of light.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: It does not. Velocities do not simply add in relativity; they combine through $w = (u+v)/(1+uv/c^2)$, and the denominator quietly holds the result below $c$ no matter how hard you push.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Set both speeds to $0.9c$: the ground speed is $0.994c$, still short of light, while the Galilean sum reads $1.8c$.
VO: Push the ball to $c$ (or the ship): the ground speed locks to exactly $c$, the invariant speed of light.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The diagnostic plots the ground speed against the ball's speed, the relativistic curve flattening against the light ceiling while the Galilean line runs off the top.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
