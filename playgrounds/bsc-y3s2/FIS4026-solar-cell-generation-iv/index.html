Solar Cell: I-V, Fill Factor and the Shockley-Queisser Limit

An interactive single-diode solar cell with the Shockley-Queisser detailed-balance limit (Shockley 1949; Shockley and Queisser 1961; Green 1981; Wurfel 2009). The ideal model I(V) = I_L - I_0[exp(V/(n V_T)) - 1] gives I_sc = I_L at V = 0 and V_oc = n V_T ln(I_L/I_0 + 1) at I = 0; the power P = V I peaks at the maximum-power point, with fill factor FF = V_mp I_mp/(V_oc I_sc) and efficiency eta = P_mpp/P_in. Panel A draws the I-V and P-V curves with I_sc, V_oc, the maximum-power point and the fill-factor rectangle for a realistic cell (a typical sub-gap voltage deficit from non-radiative recombination, so the knee is visible); a load point sweeps from short circuit to open circuit. Panel B rains photons above the gap onto the cell, generating electron-hole pairs and the photocurrent. Panel C is the radiative detailed-balance Shockley-Queisser efficiency versus bandgap (a blackbody sun diluted to the chosen incident power, recombination from the cell 300 K blackbody), peaking near 30% at about 1.3 eV, with the realistic cell efficiency shown below the limit. The short-circuit current, the open-circuit voltage capped below the bandgap, the maximum-power point, and the Shockley-Queisser efficiency peak near 1.3 eV are the physical content. Reference: Shockley and Queisser 1961; Wurfel, Physics of Solar Cells (2009).
