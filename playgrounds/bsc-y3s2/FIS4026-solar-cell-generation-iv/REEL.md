## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The ideal model I(V) = I L - I 0[exp(V/(n V T)) - 1] gives I sc = I L at V = 0 and V oc = n V T ln(I L/I 0 + 1) at I = 0; the power P = V I peaks at the maximum-power point, with fill factor FF = V mp I mp/(V oc I sc) and efficiency eta = P mpp/P in. Panel A draws the I-V and P-V curves with I sc, V oc, the maximum-power point and the fill-factor rectangle for a realistic cell (a typical sub-gap voltage deficit from non-radiative recombination, so the knee is visible); a load point sweeps from short circuit to open circuit.
