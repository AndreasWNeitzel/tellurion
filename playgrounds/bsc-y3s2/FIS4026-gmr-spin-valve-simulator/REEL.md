## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: In the metallic case the two-current model (Mott 1936; Baibich et al. 1988) treats spin-up and spin-down electrons as independent parallel channels, giving a parallel-state resistance R P = 2 R up R dn/(R up + R dn) and an antiparallel-state resistance R AP = (R up + R dn)/2, so R P <= R AP always (the arithmetic-harmonic mean inequality) and GMR = (R AP - R P)/R P = (R up - R dn) 2/(4 R up R dn) = beta 2/(1 - beta 2) with channel asymmetry beta.
