Toy parton distribution functions for the proton

What you are seeing: sketches of the longitudinal momentum fractions $x f_i(x)$ carried by each parton species. Valence quarks peak near $x \sim 0.2$, sea quarks dominate at small $x$, and the gluon carries the bulk of the proton momentum (about half at $Q^2 \sim 10$ GeV²). Source: Griffiths-Particles Ch. 9 (`griffiths-particles`).
