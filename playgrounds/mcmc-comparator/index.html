MCMC sampler comparator

Three Markov-chain Monte Carlo samplers run in parallel on a chosen 2D target density. Each sampler lays down its accepted-state trail in a distinct categorical colour; the contour map under the trails shows the target. The right-hand panel reports per-sampler acceptance, effective sample size, and a Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic against the analytic marginal where available. Switch the target or any sampler to see how mixing behaviour changes.
