Rotation curve explorer

A model spiral galaxy decomposed into three mass components: Hernquist bulge, Miyamoto-Nagai disk (z = 0), and NFW halo. The synthetic "observed" rotation curve is generated at a fixed set of "true" parameters with Gaussian noise of 4 km/s; the four sliders adjust the masses and the halo concentration. The chi-squared readout tells you how well the model fits the data. The lowest chi-squared sits at the true parameters (the Reset button restores them).
