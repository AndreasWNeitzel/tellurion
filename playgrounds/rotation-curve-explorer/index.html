Rotation curve explorer

A model spiral galaxy seen from above (left panel) and its rotation curve seen from the side (right panel). The galaxy carries the same visible matter in every case, namely a bulge plus a Miyamoto-Nagai disk. What changes is the model for the unseen mass. Switch between the three options to compare. The synthetic observation set (dots with error bars on the inset) was drawn from the dark-matter model with 6 km/s Gaussian noise; only one of the three model curves is consistent with it at R > 10 kpc.
