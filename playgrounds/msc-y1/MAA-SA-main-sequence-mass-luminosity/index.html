Main-sequence mass-luminosity relation

What you are seeing: the steep dependence of stellar luminosity on mass on the main sequence, approximated by piecewise power laws. A 2 $M_\odot$ star is $\sim 16 \times$ brighter than the Sun; a 10 $M_\odot$ star is $\sim 5000 \times$ brighter. Lifetime scales as $M / L$. Source: Carroll-Ostlie Ch. 7 (`carroll-ostlie`).
