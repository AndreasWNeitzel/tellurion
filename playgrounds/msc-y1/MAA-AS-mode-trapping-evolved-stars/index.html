Mode trapping in evolved stars

What you are seeing: a buoyancy-frequency glitch (sharp localised variation of $N$) imprints a periodic modulation on the g-mode period spacing $\Delta P(P)$. The modulation amplitude $A$ measures glitch strength; the modulation period $P_{\rm trap}$ measures the glitch position. Source: Mosser et al. 2018 (`mosser2018-trap`); Aerts et al. Ch. 3.4 (`aerts-asteroseism`).
