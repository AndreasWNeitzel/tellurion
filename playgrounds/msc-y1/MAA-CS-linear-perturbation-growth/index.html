Linear density-perturbation growth

What you are seeing: the linear growth of matter density perturbations in a flat $\Lambda$CDM universe. $\delta \propto a$ during matter domination; Lambda freezes the growth at late times. The growth factor $f(a) = d\ln\delta/d\ln a \approx \Omega_m(a)^{0.55}$ tracks the transition. Source: Liddle Ch. 12 (`liddle-cosmology`).
