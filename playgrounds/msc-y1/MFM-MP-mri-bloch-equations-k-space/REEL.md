## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The Bloch equations are solved analytically in the rotating frame: after a 90-degree pulse the magnetization precesses while the transverse component decays with $T_2$ and the longitudinal component recovers to $M_0$ with $T_1$, tracing a spiral on the Bloch sphere and producing the free induction decay whose Fourier transform is a Lorentzian. A brain-like phantom is imaged with the spin-echo equation $S \sim \rho (1 - e^{-TR/T_1}) e^{-TE/T_2}$ or the spoiled gradient-echo Ernst-angle equation; the image is transformed to k-space and reconstructed by a 2D inverse Fourier transform, and discarding the outer k-space lines blurs it.
