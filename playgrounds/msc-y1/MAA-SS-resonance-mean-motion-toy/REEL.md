## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The slow resonant angle phi obeys a pendulum equation, phi-double-dot proportional to -sin(phi), so an asteroid is either trapped and librating about the resonance or circulating past it; near strong resonances the chaotic pumping of eccentricity drives the body onto a planet-crossing orbit and it is removed, carving the Kirkwood gap over millions of years.
