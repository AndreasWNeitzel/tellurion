Kirkwood gaps: mean-motion resonances with Jupiter

What you are seeing: the asteroid belt (orange dots) has gaps where Jupiter's 2:1, 3:1, 5:2 etc. mean-motion resonances pump up asteroid eccentricities until they leave the belt. The dashed lines mark the resonance semi-major axes. Source: Murray-Dermott Solar System Dynamics Ch. 8 (`murray-dermott`).
