Baryon acoustic oscillations

What you are seeing: a high-density perturbation in the early universe (photons + baryons + dark matter). Photons drag baryons outward as a pressure wave at $c_s \approx c/\sqrt{3(1+R)}$. At recombination, the wave freezes at the "sound horizon" $\sim 150$ Mpc, leaving a baryon shell around each density peak. Source: Liddle Ch. 11 (`liddle-cosmology`).
