## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: In the tight-winding (WKB) limit a small-amplitude density wave in a differentially rotating stellar disk obeys the Lin-Shu dispersion relation (omega - m Omega) 2 = kappa 2 - 2 pi G Sigma |k| F(s, Q), linking the pattern frequency to the radial wavenumber k through the epicyclic frequency kappa, the surface density Sigma and the reduction factor F.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: A single dimensionless number, Toomre''s Q = kappa sigma R / (3. 36 G Sigma), decides stability: for Q > 1 the disk is stable to axisymmetric perturbations and only certain wavelengths support travelling waves, while Q < 1 lets perturbations grow.
Caption: the physics, simply
