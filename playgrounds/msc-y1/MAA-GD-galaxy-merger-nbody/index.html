Galaxy Merger N-Body

Two multi-component galaxies (a dominant dark Hernquist halo plus a stellar disk each) fall together as one true self-gravitating system. The in-plane gravity is solved self-consistently with an isolated (vacuum) FFT particle-mesh Poisson solver; every particle also has a real dynamical vertical motion (the isothermal-sheet field), so the disk is a genuine 3D body that thickens when the encounter shocks it, shown with an inclined perspective camera. Dynamical friction, tidal tails and coalescence all emerge from the particle dynamics, with no analytic cores or merge events. The right panel tracks the stars in the energy vs angular-momentum plane in the surviving primary's frame, where the shredded companion leaves the Gaia-Enceladus / Sausage signature. Sliders set the two masses, the impact parameter, the closing speed and the angle of attack; the encounter only restarts when you press Relaunch.
