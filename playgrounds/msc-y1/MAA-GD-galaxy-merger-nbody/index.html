Galaxy Merger N-Body

Two galaxies, each a dominant dark-matter halo around a stellar component (a spiral disk and a small diffuse dwarf), fall together in three dimensions under their mutual gravity. Nothing about the merger is scripted: the companion raises a trailing wake, loses orbital energy to dynamical friction, sinks, and is tidally stretched into bridges and tails that relax into one remnant. The angle of attack sets how the companion comes in, from edge-on to the disk (0 deg) to face-on (90 deg), alongside the two masses, impact parameter and closing speed. Drag to orbit the view, scroll to zoom, Shift-drag to pan. The right panel tracks the stars in the energy versus angular-momentum plane in the surviving galaxy's rest frame, where the shredded companion settles into the low-angular-momentum clump that marks the Gaia-Enceladus / Sausage debris in the real Milky Way halo. The encounter only restarts when you press Relaunch.
