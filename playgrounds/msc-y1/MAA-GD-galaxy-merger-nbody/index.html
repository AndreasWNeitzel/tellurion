Galaxy Merger N-Body

Two multi-component galaxies (a dominant dark Hernquist halo plus a stellar disk each) fall together as one fully three-dimensional self-gravitating system. Gravity is the Barnes-Hut octree (O(N log N), no grid, no periodic box, no analytic cores, no merge event), the standard isolated-system N-body method; every particle has a true 3D position and velocity, so the in-fall, tidal tails, bridges, dynamical-friction inspiral and coalescence all emerge from the 3D dynamics, solved per frame at 60fps. The angle of attack is the inclination of the companion's approach to the primary disk plane: edge-on at 0 deg, face-on at 90 deg. Drag to orbit the real point cloud, scroll to zoom, Shift-drag to pan. The right panel tracks the stars in the energy vs angular-momentum plane in the surviving primary's frame, where the shredded companion leaves the Gaia-Enceladus / Sausage signature. The encounter only restarts when you press Relaunch.
