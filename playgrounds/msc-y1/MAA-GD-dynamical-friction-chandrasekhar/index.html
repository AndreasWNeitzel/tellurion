Chandrasekhar dynamical friction

What you are seeing: a massive perturber (cyan) moves through a Maxwellian background; particles overshooting the perturber and accumulating behind it create an overdensity that pulls the perturber backward (dynamical friction). The friction force peaks near $v \sim \sigma$ and decays as $1/v^2$ at high $v$. Source: Binney-Tremaine Ch. 8 (`binney-tremaine`).
