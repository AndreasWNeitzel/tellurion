Stellar habitable zone

What you are seeing: the habitable-zone band of a star with stellar radius $R_\star$ and effective temperature $T_\text{eff}$. Stellar luminosity $L = 4 \pi R_\star^2 \sigma T_\text{eff}^4$ sets the incident flux $S(d) = L / (4 \pi d^2)$ at distance $d$ from the star. The continuous habitable zone (Kasting et al. 1993, recent estimates) corresponds to $S$ between $1.37\,S_\odot$ (inner edge, runaway greenhouse) and $0.354\,S_\odot$ (outer edge, maximum greenhouse).

The Sun-like default places the HZ between 0.85 and 1.68 AU; Earth at 1 AU sits comfortably inside. A cool M-dwarf at $T_\text{eff} = 3000$ K and $R_\star = 0.3\,R_\odot$ has its HZ at about 0.05-0.10 AU; this is why Proxima Centauri b at 0.05 AU is a candidate habitable world.
