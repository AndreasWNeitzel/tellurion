# Reel script: Aperture Synthesis on the UV Plane

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A radio interferometer images the sky one baseline at a time as Earth rotates.
Caption: A radio interferometer images the sky one…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Five real telescopes (ALMA, VLA, Effelsberg, Metsahovi, JCMT) trace UV-plane arcs as Earth rotates; the dirty image of a three-source sky model sharpens in real time via direct inverse Fourier transform.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Drag a telescope to a new latitude, watch its arcs change, and see the resolution improve.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)

VO: Compare the diagnostic plot against the live scene.
Caption: your turn
