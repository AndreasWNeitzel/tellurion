## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The creation operator raises a state (a-dagger|n> = sqrt(n+1)|n+1>) and the annihilation operator lowers it (a|n> = sqrt(n)|n-1>). Switch between bosons, which climb the ladder without limit and obey [a, a-dagger] = 1, and fermions, which saturate at |1> by the Pauli principle and obey {a, a-dagger} = 1.
