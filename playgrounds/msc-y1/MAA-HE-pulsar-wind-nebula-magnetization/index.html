Pulsar wind nebula: termination shock and magnetization

What you are seeing: a pulsar wind nebula. The pulsar's spin-down luminosity drives a relativistic wind that terminates at a shock $R_{TS} = \sqrt{L_{sd} / (4\pi c P_{ext})}$. The magnetization $\sigma = U_B / U_{\rm particle}$ controls the post-shock dynamics. Source: Kennel-Coroniti 1984.
