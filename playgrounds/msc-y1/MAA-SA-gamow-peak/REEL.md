# Reel script: The Gamow Peak

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A star is a furnace that runs through a keyhole. Almost no collision is energetic enough to fuse, and yet the Sun shines.
Caption: how does the Sun fuse anything?

## Beat 2, the two curves (3 to 12s)
VO: Hot nuclei follow this blue curve: plenty at low energy, almost none in the fast tail. Tunneling through the Coulomb barrier follows the gold curve: hopeless at low energy, easier when you are fast.
Caption: too slow to fuse, too rare to be fast

## Beat 3, the peak (12 to 22s)
VO: Multiply them and you get the magenta Gamow peak. Fusion only happens in this narrow window, far out on the tail, where both curves are tiny. For proton-proton in the Sun that is about six kilo-electron-volts.
Caption: the Gamow peak: the only place fusion happens

## Beat 4, temperature (22 to 31s)
VO: Now push the temperature. The peak slides right and the rate underneath jumps by orders of magnitude for a few percent more heat. That steep slope is the thermostat that keeps a star stable.
Caption: a few percent hotter, orders of magnitude faster

## Beat 5, heavier fuel and CTA (31 to 40s)
VO: Switch to carbon burning: bigger charges, a taller barrier, a peak at mega-electron-volts that only ignites in a much hotter core. Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
