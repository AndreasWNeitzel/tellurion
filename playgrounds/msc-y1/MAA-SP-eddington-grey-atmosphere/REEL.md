# Reel script: Eddington Grey Atmosphere

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Starlight is not emitted from a surface but from a fuzzy layer where the gas goes from opaque to transparent; the simplest model of that layer predicts the photosphere and limb darkening.

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Assuming a frequency-independent (grey) opacity and radiative equilibrium, the Eddington approximation closes the moment equations and gives the temperature run T 4(tau) = (3/4) T eff 4 (tau + 2/3), so the effective temperature is reached at optical depth tau = 2/3 (the photosphere) rather than at tau = 0.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Solving the transfer equation with the Eddington-Barbier relation yields the emergent intensity I(0, mu) proportional to (2/3 + mu): brighter looking straight down into the star (mu = 1, disk centre) and dimmer toward the limb (small mu), which is limb darkening. The playground shows the T(tau) profile and the angular intensity as T eff is varied.
Caption: the physics, simply
