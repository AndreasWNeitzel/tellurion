CMB temperature power spectrum (toy)

What you are seeing: the angular power spectrum $D_\ell = \ell(\ell+1)C_\ell/2\pi$ of CMB temperature fluctuations. The first acoustic peak at $\ell \sim 220$ measures the geometry; peak ratios constrain $\Omega_b$; the damping tail measures Silk damping. Toy model only. Source: Liddle Ch. 12 (`liddle-cosmology`).
