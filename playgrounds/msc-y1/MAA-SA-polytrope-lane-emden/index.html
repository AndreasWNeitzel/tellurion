Lane-Emden polytrope

What you are seeing: the dimensionless density profile $\theta(\xi)$ of a polytropic star satisfying $\theta'' + (2/\xi) \theta' + \theta^n = 0$, with $\theta(0) = 1$ and $\theta'(0) = 0$. The first zero $\xi_1$ marks the stellar surface. Three indices admit closed-form solutions ($n = 0, 1, 5$); others including $n = 3/2$ (degenerate non-rel, brown dwarfs) and $n = 3$ (relativistic-limit white dwarf, Chandrasekhar) require numerical RK4.

The right panel marks $\xi_1$ for the chosen $n$. Known values cross-checked: $\xi_1 = \sqrt{6}$ for $n = 0$, $\xi_1 = \pi$ for $n = 1$, $\xi_1 \approx 3.6537$ for $n = 1.5$, $\xi_1 \approx 6.8969$ for $n = 3$, and $\xi_1 \to \infty$ for $n = 5$ (infinite radius).
