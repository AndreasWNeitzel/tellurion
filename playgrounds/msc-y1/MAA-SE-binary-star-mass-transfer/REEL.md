## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The corotating Roche potential gives the five Lagrange points and the figure-eight critical equipotential through the inner point L1; the Eggleton (1983) formula gives the volume-equivalent Roche-lobe radius. Conservative mass transfer holds the total mass and the orbital angular momentum fixed, so the separation scales as a ~ (M1 M2)^-2 and the orbit shrinks while the donor is the more massive star, reaches a minimum at q = 1, then widens.
