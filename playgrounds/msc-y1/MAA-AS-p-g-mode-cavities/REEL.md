## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: A pulsating cross-section shows the mode displacement field (degree ℓ\ellℓ): large where the mode propagates, evanescent where it cannot. A low-frequency mode is trapped in the radiative-core g-cavity, a high-frequency mode in the acoustic-envelope p-cavity, and an intermediate one is a mixed mode in both.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Start low (omega near 1): the mode is a g-mode, trapped in the core where omega is below both N and S_l; the cross-section rings in the centre.
VO: Raise omega past about 3: it becomes a p-mode, trapped in the envelope where omega is above both frequencies; the ripples move to the outer shell.
Caption: your turn
