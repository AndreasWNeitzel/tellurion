p- and g-mode cavities (propagation diagram)

What you are seeing: a propagation diagram for a stellar model. A mode of frequency $\omega$ is propagating wherever $\omega \gt $ both $N$ and $S_\ell$ (p-cavity, blue) or $\omega \lt $ both (g-cavity, orange). Between, the mode is evanescent. Source: Aerts-Christensen-Dalsgaard-Kurtz Ch. 3 (`aerts-asteroseism`).
