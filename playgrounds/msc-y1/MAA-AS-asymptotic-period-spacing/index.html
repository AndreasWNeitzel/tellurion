Asymptotic period spacing in red giants

What you are seeing: evolved-star $\ell = 1$ g modes form a comb with nearly equal period spacings $\Pi_1$. The value of $\Pi_1$ distinguishes RGB stars ($\Pi_1 \sim 60$-100 s) from red-clump stars ($\Pi_1 \sim 200$-300 s), because helium-burning cores rearrange the buoyancy frequency $N$. Source: Aerts-Christensen-Dalsgaard-Kurtz Ch. 3.4 (`aerts-asteroseism`).
