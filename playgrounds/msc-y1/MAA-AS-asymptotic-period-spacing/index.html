What you are seeing: a vertical cross-section of a red giant with the buoyancy-trapped ℓ=1\ell = 1ℓ=1 g-mode oscillating inside its core cavity. The cavity is where N(r)>0N(r) > 0N(r)>0 (radiative buoyancy region); a convective core or envelope shuts it off.
