Atmospheric speckle statistics

What you are seeing: a short-exposure stellar image through atmospheric turbulence. The pattern has $N \sim (D/r_0)^2$ speckles, each of size $\sim \lambda / D$. Re-roll to see different realizations: the ensemble follows negative-exponential intensity statistics. Source: Roddier, Adaptive Optics (`hardy-ao`).
