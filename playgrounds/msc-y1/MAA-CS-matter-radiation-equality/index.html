Matter, radiation, dark energy: equality and dominance

What you are seeing: the three components of the cosmological energy density plotted on a log-log scale against the scale factor $a$ (with $a = 1$ today). Matter dilutes as $\rho_m \propto a^{-3}$, radiation as $\rho_r \propto a^{-4}$, and the cosmological constant $\rho_\Lambda$ stays constant.

Two crossovers are marked: matter-radiation equality at $a_{eq} = \Omega_r / \Omega_m \approx 2.9 \times 10^{-4}$ ($z_{eq} \approx 3410$), and matter-Lambda equality at $a_{m\Lambda} = (\Omega_m / \Omega_\Lambda)^{1/3} \approx 0.77$. Below $a_{eq}$ the universe is radiation-dominated; between $a_{eq}$ and $a_{m\Lambda}$ it is matter-dominated; today (just past $a_{m\Lambda}$) it is Lambda-dominated and accelerating.
