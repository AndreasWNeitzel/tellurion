Bayesian coin update

Conjugate Bayesian inference for the bias theta of an unfair coin. Prior: Beta(alpha0, beta0). Data: k heads observed in n flips. Posterior: Beta(alpha0 + k, beta0 + n - k). The plot overlays the prior (cat-1), the likelihood (theta to the k times (1 - theta) to the n - k, renormalized for display), and the posterior (cat-3). Drag k and n to see the posterior sharpen.
