Lorenz attractor

The Lorenz 1963 system, the canonical example of deterministic chaos. Three coupled ODEs in (x, y, z); the (x, z) projection is rendered as the trajectory traces out the strange attractor's two-lobed butterfly. A live tangent-vector estimate gives the running maximum Lyapunov exponent (analytic value 0.9056 at the classical sigma=10, rho=28, beta=8/3). Tune the parameters to escape the chaotic regime (rho below 24.74 gives stable spirals; very large rho gives periodic windows).
