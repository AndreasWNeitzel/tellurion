# Reel script: Phased Array Beam Steering

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: This radar sweeps the whole sky and nothing is turning. Watch the beam, then watch the antennas.
Caption: it scans without moving

## Beat 2, the mechanism (3 to 12s)
VO: Here are eight antennas in a row, all sent the same signal. The only difference is a steady phase step from one to the next, the colours along the bottom. The waves add up in phase in just one direction.
Caption: same signal, a phase step per element

## Beat 3, steering (12 to 22s)
VO: Change that phase step and the direction of agreement moves, so the beam swings. No motor, no dish, just electronics repointing in microseconds. This is how 5G and modern radar aim.
Caption: phase steers the beam electronically

## Beat 4, beamwidth and side lobes (22 to 31s)
VO: Add more antennas and the beam gets sharper and the side lobes drop. The decibel plot below tracks the beamwidth and the strongest stray lobe.
Caption: more elements, sharper beam

## Beat 5, grating lobes and CTA (31 to 40s)
VO: Spread the elements past one wavelength and a second full-strength beam appears in the wrong direction, a grating lobe. That is why real arrays sit near half a wavelength. Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
