Mandel-Agol transit light curve

What you are seeing: a planet of radius $R_p$ transits across a star of radius $R_s$. The analytic Mandel-Agol model gives the in-transit flux as a function of normalized separation $z = d / R_s$ and radius ratio $p = R_p / R_s$. Quadratic limb darkening deepens the center of the dip. Source: Mandel & Agol 2002 (`mandelagol2002`).
