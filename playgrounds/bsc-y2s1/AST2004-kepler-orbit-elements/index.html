Keplerian orbit elements

What you are seeing: the six classical orbital elements parameterize a Kepler orbit. Semi-major axis $a$ and eccentricity $e$ set the shape; inclination $i$, longitude of ascending node $\Omega$, and argument of periapsis $\omega$ orient the orbit in 3D. Source: Carroll-Ostlie Ch. 2 (`carroll-ostlie`).
