## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The monatomic chain has exactly N modes at omega n = 2 sqrt(K/m) sin(n pi / 2(N+1)), each a standing wave with n-1 internal nodes. Switching to a diatomic chain with two alternating spring constants splits the spectrum into an acoustic and an optical branch separated by a zone-boundary band gap that closes exactly when the springs are equal.
