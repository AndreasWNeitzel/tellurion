The Lyman series (nℓ=1n_\ell = 1nℓ​=1) lives in the UV and converges at 91.2 nm; the Balmer series (nℓ=2n_\ell = 2nℓ​=2) is in the visible and converges at 365 nm; the Paschen series (nℓ=3n_\ell = 3nℓ​=3) lies in the near IR.
