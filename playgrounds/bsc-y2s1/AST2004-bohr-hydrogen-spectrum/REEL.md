## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: It is restricted to a ladder of levels, E_n = -13. 6 eV over n squared, crowding together as they climb toward zero.

VO: Raise the upper level and watch the line shift toward the blue and crowd toward the series limit.
Caption: your turn
