Larmor radiation pattern (non-relativistic)

What you are seeing: an electron oscillating along the vertical axis radiates with the classic $\sin^2\theta$ "donut" pattern: nothing along the acceleration axis, maximum perpendicular to it. The total power $P_{tot} = q^2 a^2 / (6\pi\varepsilon_0 c^3)$. Source: Griffiths E&M Ch. 11 (`griffiths-em`).
