q-state Potts model on a 2D square lattice

What you are seeing: a generalization of the Ising model where each site holds one of qqq colors (instead of just two spin values). Like-colored neighbors bind with energy −J δ(si,sj)-J\,\delta(s_i, s_j)−Jδ(si​,sj​); the system minimizes this energy by forming large monochromatic patches at low temperature and disordering into salt-and-pepper noise at high temperature.

The transition temperature is Tc(q)=1/ln⁡(1+q)T_c(q) = 1 / \ln(1 + \sqrt q)Tc​(q)=1/ln(1+q​) (Wu 1982). For q=2q = 2q=2 this reduces to the Ising model and the transition is second-order: the order parameter MMM drops smoothly to zero at TcT_cTc​. For q≥5q \geq 5q≥5 the transition becomes first-order and you see latent-heat jumps in the energy across TcT_cTc​. The q=3q = 3q=3 and q=4q = 4q=4 cases sit in between with a marginal second-order behavior.
