Stellar spectrum: blackbody continuum and absorption lines

What you are seeing: the Planck continuum (yellow) shifts with TTT (Wien's law); on top, characteristic stellar absorption lines (H Balmer, Ca II H&K, Na D, Mg b) cut into the spectrum. Sliding TTT lets you sweep through OBAFGKM-type stars and see the peak migrate from UV through visible to IR. Source: Carroll-Ostlie Ch. 3, 9 (`carroll-ostlie`).
