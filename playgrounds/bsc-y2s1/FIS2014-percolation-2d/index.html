2D site percolation

What you are seeing: a square lattice where each site is independently "occupied" with probability ppp. As ppp rises past a critical value, a single giant connected cluster appears that spans the lattice from top to bottom (the percolating cluster). Below the critical ppp, only small isolated islands.
