Radial-velocity curve and orbit

What you are seeing: a spectroscopic binary or planet-host star. The orbit drawn at left projects to the radial-velocity curve at right. Eccentric orbits produce asymmetric, "sawtooth-like" RV traces; circular orbits give pure sinusoids. The amplitude $K$ measures the line-of-sight projected speed. Source: Carroll-Ostlie Ch. 7 (`carroll-ostlie`).
