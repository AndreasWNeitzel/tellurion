Elastic Waves: P and S Modes in a Solid

A 2D isotropic solid obeying $\rho\,\ddot{\mathbf u} = (\lambda+\mu)\nabla(\nabla\!\cdot\mathbf u) + \mu\nabla^2\mathbf u$. A source launches a compressional P wave at $v_P=\sqrt{(\lambda+2\mu)/\rho}$ and a shear S wave at $v_S=\sqrt{\mu/\rho}$. The medium is coloured by its divergence (compression) and a reference grid shows it straining; the red and blue rings are the analytic P and S wavefronts. The side panel is the seismogram at a station, where the P arrival precedes the S arrival by $d(1/v_S-1/v_P)$. Lower the shear modulus toward zero and the S wave disappears.
