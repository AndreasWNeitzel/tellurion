Thermodynamic Engine Simulator (Hero)

A piston-cylinder engine running a chosen ideal-gas cycle. The gas molecules (left) speed up and slow as the temperature changes and the piston tracks the volume; the P-V loop traces in the centre; the energy-flow diagram on the right splits $Q_{\rm hot}$ into work and $Q_{\rm cold}$ with the live efficiency. Carnot, Otto, Diesel and Stirling cycles; reverse it to run a refrigerator.
