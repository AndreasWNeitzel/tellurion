Skin effect: AC field in a conductor

What you are seeing: the electric field $E(z, t) = E_0 e^{-z/\delta} \cos(\omega t - z/\delta)$ inside a conductor. The skin depth $\delta = \sqrt{2/(\omega \mu \sigma)}$ shrinks with frequency. Cu at 60 Hz: 8.5 mm; at 1 GHz: 2 μm. Source: Griffiths E&M Ch. 9 (`griffiths-em`).
