The Huygens Construction

Every point on a wavefront is itself a source of a spherical wavelet; the envelope of the wavelets is the wavefront an instant later. Replace a wavefront by $N$ secondary point sources and watch the superposition rebuild it: a flat aperture diffracts into the single-slit pattern (the Fraunhofer amplitude is $\operatorname{sinc}(\pi a \sin\theta/\lambda)$, with zeros at $\sin\theta = m\lambda/a$), while a concave arc of equal-phase wavelets converges to a focus. The side panel compares the $N$-source far field with the analytic sinc envelope.
