Relativistic radiation: parallel and circular cases

What you are seeing: the angular distribution of radiation from a charge accelerated either parallel to or perpendicular to its velocity. As $\gamma \to \infty$ the radiation collimates into a forward cone of opening angle $\sim 1/\gamma$, which is the synchrotron beaming effect. The cyan circle marks the $1/\gamma$ opening angle. Source: Jackson 3e Ch. 14 (`jackson3e`).
