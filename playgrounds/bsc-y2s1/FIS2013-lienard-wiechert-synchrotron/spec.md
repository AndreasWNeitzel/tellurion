# Lienard-Wiechert beaming
Relativistic radiation collimates into a forward cone of opening angle $\sim 1/\gamma$. Two limits (a parallel and a perpendicular to v) are shown. Source: Jackson 3e Ch. 14 (`jackson3e`).
