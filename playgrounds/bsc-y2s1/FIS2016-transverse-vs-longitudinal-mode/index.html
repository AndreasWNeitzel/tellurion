Transverse vs longitudinal modes on a 1D chain

What you are seeing: a 1D chain of masses connected by springs supports both transverse waves (particles bob perpendicular to the chain) and longitudinal waves (particles compress along the chain). Both share the dispersion relation $\omega(k) = 2\sqrt{K/m}|\sin(ka/2)|$. Source: Crawford Ch. 5 (`crawford-waves`).
