Tunneling: rectangular barrier

A 1D Schrodinger particle of energy E incident on a square barrier of height V_0 and width a. Below the barrier (E < V_0) the wave decays exponentially inside but a finite transmission T(E) leaks through; above the barrier (E > V_0) classical mechanics says T = 1 but quantum mechanics shows oscillations with resonance dips at kappa a = n pi. Sweep E to trace out T(E); the dashed curve is the classical step function for comparison.
