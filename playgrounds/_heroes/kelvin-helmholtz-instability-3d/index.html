Kelvin-Helmholtz instability

What you are seeing: two fluid layers sliding past one another at a sheared interface. The Stuart 1967 streamfunction $\psi = -\ln(\cosh y + A \cos x)$ generates the exact cats-eye flow as a function of the amplitude $A$. As $A$ increases from 0, the interface rolls up into a row of vortices and the layers mix. Source: Chandrasekhar, Hydrodynamic and Hydromagnetic Stability, Ch. 11 (chandrasekhar-hydro).
