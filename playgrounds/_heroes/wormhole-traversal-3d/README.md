# Wormhole Traversal (Hero)

What it shows: a Morris-Thorne traversable wormhole in the zero-tidal
Ellis form, ray-marched in real time. Every pixel integrates a light
ray through the curved metric, so the throat genuinely shows a second
universe warped into a circular window, and flying through swaps the
sky. The funnel panel is the exact embedding z = b0 asinh(l/b0). A ray
passes to the other side only if its impact parameter is below the
throat radius; otherwise it scatters back.

Honest framing: this is a geometry explorer. A real traversable
wormhole would need exotic matter (negative energy density) that may
not exist; the playground demonstrates the spacetime shape, not the
engineering.

What to look for: "approach" grows the far-sky window; "traverse"
swaps the sky; "orbit the mouth" looks around the rim. Widen b0 to
open the window.

Controls: throat radius b0, ship position l (fly-through), look yaw,
tidal scaling, presets, play / pause / reverse / far-side.
