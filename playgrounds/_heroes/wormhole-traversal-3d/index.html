Wormhole Traversal

A Morris-Thorne traversable wormhole (the zero-tidal Ellis geometry $ds^2=-dt^2+d\ell^2+(b_0^2+\ell^2)\,d\Omega^2$), ray-marched in real time. Looking into the throat you see the far universe warped into a circular window; fly through and the sky swaps. The funnel panel is the real embedding $z(\ell)=b_0\,\mathrm{arcsinh}(\ell/b_0)$. This is a geometry explorer: a real traversable wormhole would require exotic matter that may not exist.
