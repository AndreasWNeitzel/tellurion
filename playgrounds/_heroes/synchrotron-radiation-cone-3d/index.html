Synchrotron radiation cone

What you are seeing: a relativistic electron of Lorentz factor $\gamma$ orbiting in a magnetic field $\vec B$. Its radiation is beamed into a forward cone of half-angle $\sim 1/\gamma$ pointing along the instantaneous velocity. The bottom panel is the observed pulse profile and synchrotron spectrum with peak at $\nu_c = (3/2) \gamma^3 \nu_L$. Source: Rybicki and Lightman, Radiative Processes in Astrophysics, Ch. 6 (rybicki-lightman).
