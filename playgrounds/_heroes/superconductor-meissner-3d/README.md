# Meissner Effect (Hero)

What it shows: a bar magnet above a superconductor. Below Tc the
superconductor expels the magnetic field (the Meissner effect),
modelled exactly by the image dipole that makes the normal field
vanish at the surface; the resulting repulsion levitates the magnet.
Field lines curve around the cold sample and thread straight through a
warm one; the glowing skin is the London penetration layer. Warm past
Tc or overfield past Hc(T) and superconductivity quenches, the magnet
drops.

What to look for: switch presets (Meissner levitation, normal,
Type-II vortex, quench by overfield); drag T/Tc up and watch the
London skin thicken until screening fails. The lower panel is the
field-versus-depth profile (exponential when cold, flat when warm).

Controls: T/Tc, applied field, lambda0, magnet moment, presets,
play / pause / cool / warm. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
