# Reel script: Map Projection Explorer

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: No flat map of a round Earth can keep area, angle, and distance all true at once: Gauss''s Theorema Egregium forbids it, and every projection picks which property to sacrifice.
Caption: No flat map of a round Earth can keep are…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Every flat map of the round Earth has to distort something: area, angle, distance, or shape.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: No projection preserves all of them. This explorer projects the globe through twelve classic map projections and overlays Tissot's indicatrix, the small ellipse that turns the invisible local distortion into something you can see.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Switch to Mercator and watch the Tissot ellipses inflate toward the poles: the indicatrices stay circular (Mercator is conformal) but their area runs away, which is why Greenland looks the size of Africa.
VO: Switch to Mollweide or Hammer: every indicatrix now has the same area (equal-area) but they squash into ellipses, so shapes are sacrificed instead.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: Drag the canvas to recentre the globe and watch the distortion pattern move with it.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
