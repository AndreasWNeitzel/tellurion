# Dark Energy and the Fate of the Universe

A comoving 3D lattice of galaxies scales with the Friedmann a(t).
Adjust Omega_m, Omega_Lambda or pick one of four canonical presets
and watch the cube expand toward heat death, accelerate toward de
Sitter, recollapse to a Big Crunch, or run away into a phantom-DE Big
Rip.

The right panel plots a(t) integrated by RK4 with turnaround detection
for closed models. The current marker (blue dot) tracks cosmic time as
it advances.

Engine: shared/js/engine/friedmann-cpu.js. Reference: Ryden,
Introduction to Cosmology, Ch. 5-6.
