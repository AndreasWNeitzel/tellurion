Earth, Moon, Sun and eclipse prediction

What you are seeing: the Sun, Earth, and Moon in a 3D heliocentric view, with the Moon's orbit tilted 5.145° from the ecliptic. The geometry is exact for the angular-size test that detects an eclipse; bodies and orbits are scaled for visualization. Scrub the time slider or hit "next solar" / "next lunar" to jump to the next eclipse. Drag to rotate the camera. Source: Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, 2nd ed. (meeus1998).
