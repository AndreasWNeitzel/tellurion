Hawking radiation and BH evaporation

What you are seeing: a Schwarzschild black hole at temperature $T_H = \hbar c^3 / (8 \pi G M k_B)$. Particle-antiparticle pairs spawn near the horizon: one quantum falls in (negative-energy mode), the other escapes carrying the Hawking flux. The right panel shows M(t) and T_H(t) over the evaporation timeline. Source: Hawking, Commun. Math. Phys. 43 (1975) 199 (hawking-1975).
