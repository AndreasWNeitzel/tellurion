Faraday rotation in magnetized plasma

What you are seeing: a linearly polarized radio wave entering a column of magnetized plasma. The polarization angle rotates by $\chi = RM\, \lambda^2$ over the path, where $RM = 8.12 \times 10^5 \int n_e B_\parallel\, \mathrm{d}z$. Multi-wavelength view shows three colors rotating by different amounts (the $\lambda^2$ signature). Source: Beck, Astron. Astrophys. Rev. 24 (2015) 4 (beck-2015-magnetic-fields).
