## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: When a linearly polarized electromagnetic wave propagates along a magnetized plasma, the two circular components have slightly different refractive indices and accumulate a phase difference, equivalent to a rotation of the linear polarization by chi(z) = RM * lambda 2.
