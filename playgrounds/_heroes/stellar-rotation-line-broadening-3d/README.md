# Stellar Rotation and Line Broadening

A rotating star Doppler-shifts each surface element's spectral line.
The observed absorption profile is the limb-darkened disk integral of
all those shifted copies. Slide v sin i from 0 to 120 km/s and watch
the line widen, hollow out, and develop the characteristic flat-bottom
rotational shape.

This is the classical observable that gives astronomers v sin i for
millions of stars from a single high-resolution spectrum.
