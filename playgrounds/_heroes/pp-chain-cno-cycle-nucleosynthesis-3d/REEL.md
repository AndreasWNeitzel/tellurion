## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Hydrogen burning in stars proceeds by two distinct nuclear-reaction networks: the proton-proton chain (4 H -> He4, four direct steps starting with p + p -> d + e+ + nu) and the CNO cycle (which uses pre-existing C, N, O nuclei as a catalyst).
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Both deliver Q = 26. 73 MeV per net He synthesis, but their temperature sensitivities differ dramatically: epsilon pp ~ T 4 while epsilon CNO ~ T 17.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Raise the core temperature: below about 18 million K the proton-proton chain dominates, above it the steeply temperature-sensitive CNO cycle takes over.
VO: Step through the stellar presets from M dwarf to O star: hotter, more massive cores run almost entirely on CNO, which is why massive stars burn so fiercely.
