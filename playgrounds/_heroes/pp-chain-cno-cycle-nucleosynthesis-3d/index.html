pp chain vs CNO cycle

What you are seeing: the two main hydrogen-burning networks in stellar cores, with their energy-generation rate compared across temperature. The pp chain (left) goes as $\varepsilon \propto T^4$; the CNO cycle (right) goes as $\varepsilon \propto T^{17}$. The cross-over is near $1.8 \times 10^7$ K. The Sun (T_core = $1.55 \times 10^7$ K) is about 99% pp; an O-star is essentially 100% CNO. Source: Kippenhahn, Weigert, Weiss, Stellar Structure and Evolution, Ch. 18 (kippenhahn-stellar-structure).
