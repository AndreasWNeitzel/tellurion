# Aurora Borealis (Dipole Trap)

Charged particles streaming in from the solar wind get trapped in
Earth's dipole magnetic field, spiral around field lines, and bounce
between mirror points. Where they dip below 600 km altitude near a
magnetic pole, they excite oxygen and emit the iconic green 558 nm
(lower aurora) and red 630 nm (higher aurora) lines.

Engine: Boris pusher (symplectic Lorentz integration) on a 1/r^3
dipole field. The Boris rotation conserves |v| exactly, so the
particle's kinetic energy is bounded forever.

Reference: Stormer, The Polar Aurora, Oxford 1955; Kivelson and
Russell, Introduction to Space Physics, Ch. 8.
