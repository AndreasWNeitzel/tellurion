Aurora borealis: charged particles in Earth's dipole field

What you are seeing: the solar wind injects charged particles into Earth's dipole magnetic field; they gyrate around field lines, bounce between magnetic mirror points, and where they dip into the upper atmosphere near the magnetic poles they excite oxygen to glow green (lower aurora) and red (higher aurora).
