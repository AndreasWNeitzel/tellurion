Greenhouse Effect (Hero)

What you are seeing: a 3D Earth in radiative balance with the Sun. Solar shortwave (cyan) streams in, the surface re-emits as IR (red), and a fraction of the IR is trapped by the atmospheric greenhouse layer. The equilibrium surface temperature T_surf is set by the balance. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Source: Pierrehumbert, Principles of Planetary Climate, CUP 2010, Ch. 4 (pierrehumbert-pp).
