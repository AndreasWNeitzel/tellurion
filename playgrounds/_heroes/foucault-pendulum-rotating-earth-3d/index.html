Foucault pendulum on a rotating Earth

What you are seeing: a pendulum's bob traces a rosette on the local floor while its plane of oscillation rotates at $\Omega_\oplus \sin\phi$. The globe shows the suspension point at the chosen latitude as Earth turns. At the pole the rosette closes in one sidereal day; at the equator the bob just swings back and forth on a single line. Source: Goldstein, Classical Mechanics, Ch. 4.10 (goldstein-mechanics); Foucault 1851.
