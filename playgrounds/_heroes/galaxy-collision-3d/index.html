Galaxy collision (Toomre antennae)

What you are seeing: two disk galaxies on a flyby trajectory, integrated with the shared 2D Barnes-Hut quadtree at $O(N \log N)$. Prograde-prograde geometry, so the tidal field pulls long tails (the iconic Toomre antennae) out of each disk during pericentre. Source: Toomre and Toomre, ApJ 178 (1972) 623 (toomretoomre1972); Barnes and Hut, Nature 324 (1986) 446.
