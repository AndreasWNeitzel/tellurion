Pulsar lighthouse

What you are seeing: a neutron star spinning about its spin axis (white). The magnetic dipole (cyan) is tilted by obliquity $\alpha$; emission cones (orange) of half-angle $\rho$ sweep around the polar circle. The observer line of sight (yellow dashed) is at inclination $i$. Whenever the cone intersects the line of sight, we see a pulse, plotted as $I(\phi)$ on the right. Source: Lorimer and Kramer, Handbook of Pulsar Astronomy, Section 3.4 (lorimer-kramer-pulsars).
