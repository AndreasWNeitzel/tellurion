# Laser Cavity (Hero)

What it shows: a gain medium between two mirrors, run by the laser
rate equations. Below a sharp pump threshold the excited atoms glow
but the cavity stays dark; above it the inversion clamps and the
cavity fills with synchronized photons that pour out as a coherent
beam. The threshold is emergent, not scripted. Arm the Q-switch to
store a huge inversion and release it as one giant pulse.

What to look for: drag the pump up through "at threshold" and watch
the beam ignite abruptly; "well above threshold" shows the inversion
readout clamping while output keeps rising. The lower panel is the
output-versus-pump curve with its characteristic kink.

Controls: pump P, mirror reflectivity, cavity length, upper-state
lifetime, presets, play / pause / reset, Fire Q-switch. Drag to
orbit, scroll to zoom.
