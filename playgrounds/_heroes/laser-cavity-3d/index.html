Laser Cavity

A gain medium between two mirrors, driven by the laser rate equations. Below the lasing threshold the excited atoms glow but the cavity holds only a faint incoherent trickle; turn the pump up and, at a sharp threshold, the cavity fills with synchronized photons and a bright coherent beam ignites through the output mirror. The threshold is not scripted, it emerges from the equations. Drag to orbit; push the pump past threshold, or arm the Q-switch for one giant pulse.
