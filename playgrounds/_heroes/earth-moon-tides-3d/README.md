# Earth-Moon-Sun Tides

A 3D Earth whose surface is displaced by the L=2 tidal bulge that
the Moon and Sun raise together. Two bulges, not one: one facing
the perturber and one antipodal, because the tidal force is the
GRADIENT of gravity across Earth's diameter, not the gravity itself.

Slide the lunar orbital phase from 0 (new moon) through 90 degrees
(first quarter) to 180 (full moon), and watch the bulge amplitude
swing between spring tides (Sun + Moon aligned, ~1.46x lunar) and
neap tides (quadrature, ~0.54x lunar).

Engine: closed-form L=2 Legendre tidal potential.

Reference: Murray and Dermott, Solar System Dynamics, Ch. 4.
