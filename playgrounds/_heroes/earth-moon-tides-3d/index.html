Earth-Moon-Sun tides

What you are seeing: Earth as a 3D sphere with the L=2 tidal bulge made visible. The Moon orbits in the equatorial plane and the Sun lies at +x. As the Moon moves, the bulge moves with it; when the Sun and Moon align (new or full moon) the bulge gets bigger (spring tide); at quadrature it gets smaller (neap tide). Source: Murray and Dermott, Solar System Dynamics, Ch. 4 (murraydermott1999).
