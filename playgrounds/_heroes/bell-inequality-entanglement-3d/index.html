Bell inequality and quantum entanglement

What you are seeing: a source emits two photons in a polarization singlet. Alice (left) measures at angle $a$, Bob (right) at $b$. The singlet correlation is $E(a, b) = -\cos(2(a-b))$; the CHSH statistic $S$ is bounded by 2 classically but reaches $2\sqrt{2}$ in quantum mechanics. Source: Bell, Physics 1 (1964) 195 (bell-1964); CHSH, PRL 23 (1969) 880 (chsh-1969).
