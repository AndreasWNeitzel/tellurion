# Magnetar Burst (Hero)

A magnetar is a young neutron star with a magnetic field of
$10^{14}$ to $10^{15}$ G, exceeding the Schwinger field
$B_{\rm QED} = 4.4 \times 10^{13}$ G. Magnetic stress fractures the
crust; reconnection events drive a hot pair fireball that radiates a
bright X-ray burst. About 30 magnetars are known in the Milky Way.

## What to look for

A glowing neutron-star surface with cyan dipole field lines arcing
out into space. Bright orange-white flashes pop up at random
locations on the surface: these are crustquake-triggered bursts. The
bottom panel records the aggregate X-ray lightcurve over the past 20
seconds, showing the characteristic short-burst spikes.

## Controls

- $\log_{10} B$ in Gauss (10 to 16). At $B \lesssim 10^{13}$ G the
  star quiets to a normal pulsar; above $B_{\rm QED}$ it becomes a
  magnetar.
- Period $P$ in seconds (typical 1 to 12 s).
- Animation speed scales burst rate.
- Preset menu: SGR 1806-20 (2004 giant flare source), AXP 1E 1841-045,
  normal pulsar.
