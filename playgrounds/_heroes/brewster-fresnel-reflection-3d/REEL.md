## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A light ray hits the interface between two media of refractive indices n1, n2 at angle of incidence theta i.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Snell s law gives the refracted angle, and the Fresnel coefficients give the reflected and transmitted amplitudes for the two linear polarizations s (perpendicular to the plane of incidence) and p (parallel). The reflectance R p of p-polarized light vanishes at the Brewster angle tan(theta B) = n2 / n1 (the polarizing angle Brewster discovered in 1815); R s never vanishes.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Sweep the incidence angle to Brewster: the p-polarized reflectance R_p drops to exactly zero, so reflected light is fully s-polarized.
VO: Go from glass to air and past the critical angle: the refracted ray vanishes and the light totally internally reflects, the principle behind optical fibres.
Caption: your turn
