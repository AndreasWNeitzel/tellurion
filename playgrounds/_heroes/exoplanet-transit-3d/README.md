# Exoplanet Transit (Hero)

What it shows: a planet on a real Keplerian orbit crosses its star
edge-on; the light curve dips by the square of the radius ratio, the
dip is U-shaped from limb darkening, and tilting the orbit shrinks
the transit to a graze, then vanishes. This is the discovery channel
behind thousands of confirmed exoplanets.

What to look for: switch presets (central, grazing, no transit, hot
Jupiter, Earth analogue). 'Edge-on' snaps the camera to the canonical
view. Hover the light curve for flux at that phase.

Controls: Rp/Rs, a/Rs, inclination, period, limb-darkening (u1, u2),
presets, play/pause/reset/edge-on. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
