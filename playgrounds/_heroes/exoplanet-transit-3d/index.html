Exoplanet Transit

A planet orbiting a star edge-on; every period its disc crosses the stellar disc and the starlight dips by a sharp fraction. The dip's depth $(R_p/R_s)^2$ tells you the planet's size, the timing tells you the period, the duration tells you the orbit geometry, and the U-shape comes from limb darkening (the limb is dimmer than the centre, so the dip is rounded). Tilt the orbital plane and the transit shrinks to a graze, then vanishes. Drag to orbit; click 'edge-on' for the canonical view.
