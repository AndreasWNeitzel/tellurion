Black-hole ringdown

What you are seeing: the merger remnant of a binary BH coalescence settling to a Kerr black hole by radiating its lowest quasinormal mode $(\ell, m, n) = (2, 2, 0)$. The horizon (oblate spheroid) wobbles with a damped sinusoid; the strain panel below shows $h(t)$ with the LIGO-style ringdown signature. Source: Berti, Cardoso, Will, Phys. Rev. D 73 (2006) 064030 (berti-cardoso-will-qnm).
