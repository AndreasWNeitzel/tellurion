# Black-Hole Ringdown (Hero)

After two black holes merge, the remnant settles to a Kerr equilibrium
by radiating a small number of damped sinusoids. The dominant one
the (l, m, n) = (2, 2, 0) mode has frequency and decay time that
depend only on the remnant mass $M$ and dimensionless spin $\chi =
a/M$ ("no hair"). LIGO and Virgo measure $f$ and $\tau$ directly
from the ringdown phase of merger signals.

## What to look for

The dark oblate spheroid is the remnant horizon, with subtle lambert
shading. The two cream-colored lobe outlines on the surface are the
$m = 2$ deformation pattern of the dominant QNM; they rotate around
the horizon with angular frequency $\omega_R$ and shrink by
$e^{-t/\tau}$. Below, the cyan strain trace $h(t)$ shows the damped
sinusoid that gravitational-wave detectors observe; the orange dashed
envelope is the exponential decay $\pm e^{-t/\tau}$.

## Controls

The mass slider runs from 5 to 200 $M_\odot$ (GW150914 remnant was 62
$M_\odot$). The spin slider runs from $\chi = 0$ (Schwarzschild) to
$\chi = 0.99$ (near-extremal). Watch the frequency monotonically
climb from $\sim 250$ Hz to $\sim 700$ Hz as $\chi$ increases at
fixed $M$; the damping time also lengthens because $|\omega_I|$
shrinks toward extremality. The Q-factor is the (essentially
$M$-independent) ratio $\omega_R / 2|\omega_I|$.
