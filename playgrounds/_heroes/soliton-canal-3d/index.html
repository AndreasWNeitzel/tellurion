Soliton Canal

A real Korteweg-de Vries canal: the water surface obeys $u_t + 6 u u_x + u_{xxx} = 0$, solved live by a Fourier pseudo-spectral integrator. A soliton is a single hump that travels without changing shape because nonlinear steepening cancels dispersion; taller solitons move faster ($c = 2a$). Launch one behind another and watch them collide and pass straight through. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click the water to inject a soliton.
