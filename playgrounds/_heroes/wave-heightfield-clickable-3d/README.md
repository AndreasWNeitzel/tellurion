# Wave heightfield (hero playground)

Click anywhere on the field to seed a Gaussian impulse. Watch the energy readout decay at the chosen $\gamma$. The grid is 96x96 with Dirichlet boundary conditions; the integrator is leapfrog with second-order centered Laplacian. The dispersion is non-dispersive in the continuum limit but slightly retarded on the discrete grid. Source: French Waves Ch. 6 (`french-waves`).
