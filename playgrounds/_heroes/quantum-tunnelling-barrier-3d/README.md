# Quantum Tunnelling (Hero)

What it shows: a wavepacket obeying the Schrodinger equation, solved by
Crank-Nicolson (probability conserved to round-off every step). The
terrain is the potential V(x); the glowing curtain is |psi|^2, coloured
by quantum phase. A classical ball with the same energy is launched
alongside: it always bounces off a barrier taller than its energy,
while the quantum packet partly tunnels through.

What to look for: compare "thin barrier" and "thick barrier" (same
height, very different transmission); lower V0 below the packet energy
and it sails over; "resonant double" transmits near-perfectly at
special energies. The norm readout stays pinned at 1.

Controls: barrier height/width, packet k0 and spread, presets,
play / pause / step / relaunch. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom,
Shift-drag the scene to sculpt the potential. The lower panel is the
analytic transmission curve T(E) with the packet energy marked.
