Quantum Tunnelling

A wavepacket obeying the Schrodinger equation $i\,\partial_t\psi = -\tfrac12\partial_x^2\psi + V(x)\,\psi$, solved by Crank-Nicolson (exactly probability-conserving). The terrain is the potential $V(x)$; the glowing curtain is the probability density $|\psi|^2$, coloured by the quantum phase. A classical ball with the same energy is launched alongside: it always bounces off a barrier taller than its energy. The quantum packet does not, it leaks through. Drag to orbit; raise the barrier and watch the transmitted fraction change.
