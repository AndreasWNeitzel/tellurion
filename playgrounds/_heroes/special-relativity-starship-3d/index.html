Relativistic Starship

Fly a starship toward light speed and see what relativity does to the sky. The throttle sets $\beta = v/c$. Stars bunch toward the bow (aberration), shift blue ahead and red astern (Doppler), and the forward field brightens (relativistic beaming): all the exact Lorentz transform of the incoming photons. The lower panel is the lab's view of your length-contracted ship, with the twin clocks drifting apart. Drag to look around the cockpit, throttle up, watch the sky collapse forward.
