# Relativistic Starship (Hero)

What it shows: the exact special-relativistic optics of flying near
light speed. As you throttle beta up, relativistic aberration bunches
the stars toward the bow, the Doppler effect turns forward stars blue
and rear stars red, relativistic beaming brightens the forward field,
your ship length-contracts and your clock runs slow. Every effect is
the Lorentz transform of the photons, not an animation.

What to look for: at 0.87 c (gamma 2) the ship clock ticks at half the
lab clock; push to 0.995 and the whole sky collapses into a small
bright blue disc ahead. Look astern (drag to turn) and the sky is
sparse and red.

Controls: throttle (beta), speed presets, pause/reset clocks, look
forward. Drag to look around the cockpit; click a star to read its
lab angle, apparent angle and Doppler shift. The lower panel is the
lab's view of your contracted ship and the twin clocks.
