Tokamak plasma confinement (3D hero)

What you are seeing: a torus of magnetic field lines, each helical: toroidal field $B_\phi = B_0 R_0 / R$ from external coils, poloidal field $B_\theta = \mu_0 I_p / (2\pi r)$ from plasma current. Helicity is $q(r) = r B_\phi / (R_0 B_\theta)$; at the edge $q_a = 2\pi a^2 B_0 / (\mu_0 R_0 I_p)$ which is ~3 for an ITER-like discharge. Source: Goedbloed-Poedts Ch. 5 (`goedbloed-plasma`).
