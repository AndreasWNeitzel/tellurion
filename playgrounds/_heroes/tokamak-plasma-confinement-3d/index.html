Tokamak Plasma Confinement 3D (Hero)

A tokamak holds a 100-million-degree fusion plasma with magnetic fields instead of walls. A toroidal vessel carries helical field lines coloured by strength $|B|$; charged particles either spiral freely along the field (passing) or get mirror-trapped on the weak-field outboard side and trace the classic banana orbits. A glowing core marks the confined plasma. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
