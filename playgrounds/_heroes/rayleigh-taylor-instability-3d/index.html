Rayleigh-Taylor instability

What you are seeing: a dense fluid (red) sitting above a lighter fluid (cyan) in a downward gravitational field. Any perturbation grows as $\exp(\sigma t)$ with $\sigma = \sqrt{A k g}$ (Atwood number $A$, wavenumber $k$, gravity $g$). Dense spikes fall, light bubbles rise. Source: Chandrasekhar, Hydrodynamic and Hydromagnetic Stability, Ch. 10 (chandrasekhar-hydro).
