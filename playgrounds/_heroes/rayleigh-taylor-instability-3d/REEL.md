## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A dense fluid sitting on top of a lighter one is unstable: the interface develops downward spikes and upward bubbles.
Caption: A dense fluid sitting on top of a lighter…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A dense fluid (density rho h) above a lighter one (rho l) in a gravity field g is unstable: any small perturbation of the interface grows exponentially with rate sigma(k) = sqrt(A k g), where A = (rho h - rho l)/(rho h + rho l) is the Atwood number.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Surface tension stabilises wavelengths smaller than the capillary length. In the nonlinear stage, dense fluid develops downward spikes that fall at terminal velocity v ~ sqrt(A g lambda); light fluid develops upward bubbles.
Caption: the physics, simply
