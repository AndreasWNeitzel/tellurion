Schwarzschild-Kerr black hole (3D hero)

What you are seeing: a black-hole schematic with three characteristic surfaces: event horizon $r_+ = M + \sqrt{M^2 - a^2}$ (black disk), photon sphere $r_{\rm ph} = 3M$ (Schwarzschild; orange ring), and outer ergosphere $r_{\rm erg}(\theta) = M + \sqrt{M^2 - a^2 \cos^2\theta}$ (purple). The inner accretion disk truncates at the ISCO, which migrates inward for prograde Kerr spin. Source: Shapiro-Teukolsky Ch. 12 (`shapiro-teukolsky`).
