BEC vortex lattice in a rotating trap

What you are seeing: a 2D Bose-Einstein condensate in a harmonic trap, rotated at $\Omega / \omega_{\rm trap}$. The cyan-magenta cloud is the Thomas-Fermi density; the black holes are singly-quantized vortices on an Abrikosov triangular lattice. Each vortex carries one quantum of circulation $h/m$. Vortex area density is $n_v = m \Omega / (\pi \hbar)$ (Feynman 1955). Source: Pitaevskii and Stringari, Bose-Einstein Condensation and Superfluidity, Ch. 11 (pitaevskii-stringari-bec).
