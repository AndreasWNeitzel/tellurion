# Cosmic Ray Air Shower (Hero)

A primary cosmic ray (proton through iron) entering the upper
atmosphere at $10^{15}$ to $10^{20}$ eV triggers a cascade of
secondary particles. The Heitler model (Matthews 2005) predicts the
shower maximum depth $X_{\max}$ and total particle count $N_{\max}$.
Different primaries develop the shower at different depths, the
basis for mass discrimination at Pierre Auger and Telescope Array.

## What to look for

Left: atmosphere column from 40 km altitude down to sea level. The
primary enters at the top, makes its first interaction at the white
dot ($X_1 = 90$ g cm$^{-2}$, $\sim 20$ km altitude). The cascade
develops downward with particles color-coded: cyan = electromagnetic,
red = hadronic, yellow = muons. Right: Gaisser-Hillas profile $N(X)$
with shower maximum, sea-level depth, and a reference iron $X_{\max}$
all marked.

## Controls

- Primary particle: proton, He-4, C-12, or Fe-56.
- $\log_{10} E_0$ (eV) from 14 to 20 (PeV "knee" to GZK cutoff).
- Depth-progress slider sweeps the shower development.
- Animation speed.

## Source

Matthews, *Astropart. Phys.* 22 (2005) 387 (Heitler-model treatment).
Gaisser, *Cosmic Rays and Particle Physics*, CUP 1990 (standard
reference). Modern mass-composition synthesis: Kampert and Unger,
*Astropart. Phys.* 35 (2012) 660.
