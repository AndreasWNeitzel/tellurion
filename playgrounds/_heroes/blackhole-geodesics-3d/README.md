# Black Hole Geodesics (Hero)

What it shows: how light and matter move near a Schwarzschild black
hole. The main scene is the reused real-time lensing shader (bent star
field, glowing photon ring, true shadow, beamed disc). The plane below
is the physics you drive: fire test photons and watch the razor-sharp
capture threshold at impact parameter b = 3 sqrt(3) M. Just outside it
a photon loops the photon sphere and escapes; just inside it spirals
through the horizon.

What to look for: drag the impact-parameter slider through
b/b_crit = 1.0 and fire repeatedly, watching escape flip to capture.
The grazing preset shows a near-full loop of the photon sphere; the
ISCO and plunge presets contrast a bound orbit with an inspiral. The
small inset is the effective potential V(r) showing why 6 M is the
innermost stable orbit.

Controls: impact parameter, spin a/M (lensing shader), fire / pause /
clear, presets. Drag to orbit the hole, scroll to zoom, click the
plane to launch a ray.
