Black Hole Geodesics

A Schwarzschild black hole, ray-marched: a lensed star field, the glowing photon ring, the true black shadow, and a Doppler-beamed accretion disc. Fire test photons in the plane below and watch the sharp threshold at impact parameter $b = 3\sqrt{3}\,M$: just outside it a photon whips around the photon sphere and escapes; just inside it spirals through the horizon. Drag to orbit the hole; click the plane to launch a ray.
