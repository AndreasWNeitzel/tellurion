Stellar pulsation modes in 3D

What you are seeing: a 3D star whose surface oscillates in the angular pattern of the spherical harmonic $Y_l^m(\theta, \phi)$, animated by $\cos(\omega t)$. Pick $(l, m)$ and watch the iconic asteroseismic mode patterns. Drag to orbit, slider to scrub time. Source: Aerts et al., Asteroseismology, Ch. 3 (aerts-asteroseism).
