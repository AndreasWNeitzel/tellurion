# Stellar Pulsation 3D

A 3D star whose surface oscillates in the angular pattern of a real
spherical harmonic Y_l^m(theta, phi), animated by cos(omega t). Pick
(l, m) and watch the canonical asteroseismic mode patterns:
- (1, 0) tilts the whole star back and forth.
- (2, 0) gives the prolate/oblate "football" oscillation.
- (2, 2) splits the sphere into four orange/blue quadrants.
- High (l, m) modes pack many nodal lines onto the surface.

Sphere surface displaced by R0 + amp * Y_l^m * cos(omega t) and colored
by signed displacement (warm out, cool in). Drag to orbit.

Reference: Aerts, Christensen-Dalsgaard and Kurtz, Asteroseismology,
Ch. 3.
