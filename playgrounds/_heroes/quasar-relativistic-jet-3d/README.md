# Quasar Relativistic Jet (Hero)

A supermassive black hole's bipolar relativistic jet, with one side
amplified by Doppler boosting and the other dimmed almost to
invisibility. The same engine, viewed at different angles, produces
the entire AGN unification zoo: blazar at $\theta_{\rm obs} \lesssim
1/\Gamma$, broad-line radio quasar in between, edge-on radio galaxy
at $\theta_{\rm obs} \sim 90^\circ$.

## What to look for

A central black hole with a photon-ring halo and orange accretion
disk; two anti-parallel relativistic jets along the rotation axis;
the approaching jet (white-blue, bright) and the counter-jet (dim red).
Moving white blobs along the jet illustrate apparent superluminal
motion. The right panel reports $\delta_\pm$, the jet/counter-jet
flux ratio, the apparent transverse speed $\beta_{\rm app}$, the
beaming half-angle $1/\Gamma$, and the geometric regime label
(blazar / radio quasar / radio galaxy).

## Controls

Slide the Lorentz factor $\Gamma$ from 1.2 to 20 (canonical jets are
$\Gamma \sim 10$). Slide the viewing angle $\theta_{\rm obs}$ from
zero (down the jet, blazar) to 90 deg (edge-on, radio galaxy). The
flux ratio $F_{\rm jet} / F_{\rm cj}$ exceeds $10^3$ at $\theta_{\rm
obs} \lesssim 20^\circ$ with $\Gamma = 10$. The animation-speed
slider sets how fast blobs traverse the jet; "hide counter-jet"
removes the dim red one for clarity.
