Quasar relativistic jet

What you are seeing: a supermassive black hole with a glowing accretion disk and a bipolar relativistic jet at bulk Lorentz factor $\Gamma$. The viewer angle $\theta_{\rm obs}$ from the jet axis controls Doppler boosting: the approaching side is amplified by $\delta_+^{3-\alpha}$ and the receding side dimmed by $\delta_-^{3-\alpha}$. Source: Rybicki and Lightman, Radiative Processes in Astrophysics, Section 4.8 (rybicki-lightman).
