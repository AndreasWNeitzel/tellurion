## Source
viewed end-on is a blazar (BL Lac or FSRQ); viewed edge-on is a radio galaxy. The relativistic motion also produces apparent superluminal proper motion v app = beta sin theta / (1 - beta cos theta), peaking at cos theta = beta with v app max = Gamma * beta c. The playground draws the black-hole disk + twin jets, lets the viewing angle slide from 0 to 90 deg, and shows the boost / counter-boost asymmetry and v app. Reference: Rybicki and Lightman, Radiative Processes in Astrophysics, Ch. 4.
