# Magnetic Reconnection at an X-point (Hero)

Two oppositely directed magnetic-field regions pushed together. The
frozen-in flux theorem normally keeps the lines apart; in the thin
diffusion layer where the resistive timescale wins, it breaks. Field
lines reconnect across the X-point, and the released magnetic tension
ejects the plasma along the sheet as twin jets at the Alfven speed.
This is the engine of solar flares, coronal mass ejections and
magnetospheric substorms.

## What to look for

Cyan field lines arch above the yellow current sheet (B pointing along
$+x$); orange lines arch below (B along $-x$). White inflow arrows
push plasma down from the top half and up from the bottom half toward
the sheet. The bright yellow band is the diffusion region; the
glowing white core is the X-point. Yellow outflow arrows show the
twin jets along the sheet. Tracer particles brighten as they approach
the sheet and stream out along it.

## Controls

Slide $\log_{10} \eta$ from $-8$ (solar-corona resistivity, $S \sim
10^8$) up to $-1$ (laboratory plasma) and watch the sheet thicken and
the X-point glow weaken because the Sweet-Parker rate is $M_A =
S^{-1/2}$. The Alfven-speed slider scales the outflow jet length.
The preset menu jumps between laboratory, solar corona and a faster
Petschek-like regime. Reset re-seeds the tracer particles.
