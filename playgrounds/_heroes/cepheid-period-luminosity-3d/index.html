Cepheid period-luminosity relation

What you are seeing: a Type I Cepheid variable star pulsating with period $P$. Left: the star (size + color from radius + $T_{\rm eff}$). Middle: the V-band lightcurve. Right: the Leavitt Law $M_V = -2.78 \log_{10} P - 1.35$ (Madore + Freedman 1991) with the current star and known Galactic calibrators. Source: Madore + Freedman, PASP 103 (1991) 933 (madore-freedman-1991).
