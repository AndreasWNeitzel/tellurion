Double-slit single-photon accumulator

What you are seeing: photons fired one at a time at a double slit. Each lands at one point, but the hit-position probability follows the classical two-slit intensity $I(y) = \operatorname{sinc}^2(\pi a \sin\theta / \lambda)\cos^2(\pi d \sin\theta / \lambda)$. The hit histogram converges to that profile as the photon count grows. Source: Hecht, Optics, Ch. 10 (hecht-optics); Tonomura 1989.
