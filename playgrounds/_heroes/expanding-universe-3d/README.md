# Expanding Universe (Hero)

What it shows: a lattice of galaxies whose separations grow with the
Friedmann scale factor a(t), integrated live from the densities you
choose. Every galaxy recedes from every other (Hubble's law) with no
centre. Matter decelerates the expansion; a closed universe ends in a
Big Crunch; dark energy makes it accelerate forever. Light pulses
carry the real redshift, 1+z = a_now / a_emit.

What to look for: switch presets and scrub cosmic time. The closed
preset stalls, reverses and collapses; the dark-energy preset thins
the lattice out. Click a galaxy to send light to you and read its
redshift. The lower panel is the scale-factor history a(t).

Controls: Omega_m, Omega_Lambda, H0, cosmic-time scrubber, presets,
play / pause / reverse / today / clear light. Drag to orbit, scroll
to zoom, click a galaxy to emit a pulse.
