Expanding Universe

A lattice of galaxies in a universe that obeys the Friedmann equation, integrated live. Every galaxy recedes from every other (Hubble's law $v = H d$) with no centre. Choose what the universe is made of: matter, dark energy, curvature. A matter universe decelerates and a closed one ends in a Big Crunch; dark energy makes the expansion accelerate. Click a galaxy to send a light pulse to you and read its redshift $1+z = a_{\rm obs}/a_{\rm emit}$. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, scrub cosmic time.
