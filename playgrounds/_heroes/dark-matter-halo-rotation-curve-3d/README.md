# Dark Matter Halo Rotation Curve

Three mass components: Hernquist bulge (gold), exponential disk (cyan),
NFW dark matter halo (purple). Each contributes to the enclosed mass
M(<r), and the circular speed v_c(r) = sqrt(G M(<r) / r) is plotted
for each component plus the total (white) and the visible-only
dashed prediction.

Toggle the halo off to see the rotation curve drop sharply outside
the disk, the classic visible-matter-only Keplerian fall. Toggle it
on and the flat plateau appears, the empirical signature of dark
matter that Rubin and Ford established in 1970.

Engine: closed-form enclosed-mass integrals (Hernquist, exponential
disk, NFW). All three are tested as invariants including the NFW
M(c*r_s) = M_DM constraint.

Reference: Navarro, Frenk and White, ApJ 462 (1996) 563; Binney and
Tremaine, Galactic Dynamics, 2nd ed., Ch. 2.
