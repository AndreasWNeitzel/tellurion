Exoplanet Interior

Pick a composition, watch the cutaway. A layered terrestrial planet (iron core, silicate mantle, optional water and gas envelope) is solved in the constant-density approximation: layer interfaces follow $R_i^3 = R_{i-1}^3 + 3 M_i / (4\pi\rho_i)$ and hydrostatic balance $dP/dr = -\rho g(r)$ gives the central pressure analytically. The 3D cutaway rotates so the user sees the layered structure; the side panels track the mass-radius curve (this planet on a family of compositions) and the pressure profile from the centre to the surface. Reference: Seager+ ApJ 669 (2007) 1279, Zapolsky and Salpeter ApJ 158 (1969) 809.
