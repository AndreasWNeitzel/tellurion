Penrose aperiodic tiling

What you are seeing: a Penrose P3 rhombus tiling built by Conway-Penrose deflation from a 10-fold "Sun" seed. Two prototiles (thick 72/108 and thin 36/144 rhombi); 10-fold local symmetry; no global periodicity. The A/B count ratio converges to the golden ratio $\phi = (1+\sqrt 5)/2 \approx 1.618$. Source: Senechal, Quasicrystals and Geometry, Ch. 6 (senechal-quasicrystals).
