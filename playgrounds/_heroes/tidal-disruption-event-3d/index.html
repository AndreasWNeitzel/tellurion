Tidal disruption event

What you are seeing: a star on a near-parabolic orbit around a SMBH crosses the tidal radius $R_T = R_\star (M_{\rm BH}/M_\star)^{1/3}$ and is stretched into a debris stream. Half of the debris falls back, producing the characteristic $\dot M \propto t^{-5/3}$ fallback rate (Rees 1988). The lightcurve below shows the resulting transient flare. Source: Rees, Nature 333 (1988) 523 (rees-1988-tde).
