# Tidal Disruption Event (Hero)

A star drifts within the tidal radius $R_T = R_\star (M_{\rm BH}/M_\star)^{1/3}$
of a supermassive black hole. The BH tidal field overwhelms the
star's self-gravity, stretching it into a debris stream. About half
the debris is unbound; the other half returns at a rate
$\dot M \propto t^{-5/3}$ (Rees 1988) and forms a transient accretion
disk that briefly outshines its host galaxy.

## What to look for

The top panel shows the orbital geometry: the SMBH at the center with
its orange photon-ring glow and (during fallback) a bright accretion
disk; the cyan dashed circle is the tidal radius; the star approaches
on a near-parabolic orbit, is stretched at $R_T$, and returns as a
spiral stream. The bottom panel is the lightcurve $L(t)$: a sharp
rise to the peak at $t_{\rm peak} \sim 41$ days (canonical TDE), then
the characteristic $t^{-5/3}$ power-law decay. A yellow dot tracks
the current time on the lightcurve.

## Controls

Slide $\log_{10} M_{\rm BH}$ from $10^4$ to $10^9$ $M_\odot$; above
$\sim 10^8 M_\odot$ the Schwarzschild radius exceeds $R_T$ and the
star is swallowed whole with no flare (the Hills mass cutoff).
$M_\star$ and $R_\star$ set the stellar parameters. The animation
speed slider auto-runs the phase sweep through approach $\to$
disruption $\to$ fallback.

## Source
