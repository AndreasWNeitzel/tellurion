# Gravitational Lensing

Drag the source behind a point-mass lens and watch the two images
merge into an Einstein ring, then split again on the other side.

The image-plane render shows the background source pattern pulled back
through the inverse lens map (the checker stripes warp dramatically
near the Einstein ring). The two yellow dots are the closed-form
solutions of the point-mass lens equation for the current source
position; their total magnification is reported in the readout.

Reference: Schneider, Ehlers, Falco, Gravitational Lenses, Ch. 5.
