Gravitational lensing

What you are seeing: a point-mass gravitational lens at the origin distorts the background. The image-plane render shows the source pattern pulled back through the inverse lens map; the two yellow dots mark the discrete image positions of a movable point source (red marker on the source plane below). Drag the canvas to move the source. Source: Schneider, Ehlers, Falco, Gravitational Lenses, Ch. 5 (sef1992).
