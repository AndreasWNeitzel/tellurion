# Cherenkov Radiation Cone

A charged particle moving through a transparent medium faster than the
phase velocity of light in that medium emits a shock wave of light: a
cone of half-angle theta_C = acos(1/(beta * n)). This is the same
geometry as a sonic boom, just for photons. Underwater reactors glow
blue because of it, and IceCube reconstructs neutrino tracks from it.

Slide beta from below threshold (no cone, wavelets stay nested) up
toward 1 (cone opens to its asymptote arccos(1/n) ≈ 41° in water).
Pick a denser medium (lead glass) to see the cone widen even further.

Engine: closed-form Cherenkov geometry; Frank-Tamm intensity factor
1 - 1/(beta*n)^2 reported as the live readout.

Reference: Jackson, Classical Electrodynamics, 3rd ed., Section 13.4;
Frank and Tamm, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR 14 (1937) 109.
