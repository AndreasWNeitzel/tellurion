Cherenkov radiation cone

What you are seeing: a charged particle traversing a transparent medium of refractive index $n$ at speed $\beta c$. Each point on its track emits a spherical light wavelet that expands at $c/n$; when $\beta n > 1$ those wavelets pile up on a cone of half-angle $\cos\theta_C = 1/(\beta n)$. Source: Jackson, Classical Electrodynamics, Section 13.4 (jackson3e).
