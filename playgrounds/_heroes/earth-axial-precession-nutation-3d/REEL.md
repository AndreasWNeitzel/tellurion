# Reel script: Earth Axial Precession + Nutation 3D

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Earth is a spinning top tilted 23.4 degrees, and the Sun and Moon torque it.
Caption: Earth is a spinning top tilted 23.4 degre…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A lit 3D Earth, tilted at its 23.4-degree obliquity, with the spin axis drawn out into space.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Because the planet bulges at the equator, the gravity of the Sun and Moon pulls on the bulge and torques the axis sideways, so instead of toppling it sweeps a slow circle, the axial precession, completing one cone every ~25,772 years at 50. 29 arcseconds per year.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Vary each control and watch the rail readouts respond.
VO: Compare the diagnostic plot against the live scene.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: Drag to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom; the readout shows the simulated year and the current axis angles.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
