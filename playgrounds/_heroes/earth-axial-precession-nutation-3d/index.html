Earth axial precession + nutation (3D hero)

What you are seeing: the Earth's rotation axis traces a slow cone with a 25,800-yr period (lunisolar precession at 50.29 arcsec/yr) overlaid with the 18.6-yr lunar-node nutation (amplitude ~9.2 arcsec in obliquity, ~17.2 arcsec in longitude). The current axis tip and its time history are shown projected onto the celestial sphere. Source: Smart, Celestial Mechanics.
