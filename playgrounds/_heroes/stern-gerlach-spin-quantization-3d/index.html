Stern-Gerlach spin quantization

What you are seeing: a beam of neutral atoms of angular momentum $J$ entering a magnet with field gradient $\mathrm{d}B_z/\mathrm{d}z$. The transverse force $F_z = \mu_z \mathrm{d}B_z/\mathrm{d}z$ deflects each atom by an amount proportional to its projection $m_J$. The screen on the right shows $2J+1$ discrete spots (quantum) or a continuous band (classical, for comparison). Source: Sakurai and Napolitano, Modern Quantum Mechanics, Sec. 1.1 (sakurai-napolitano).
