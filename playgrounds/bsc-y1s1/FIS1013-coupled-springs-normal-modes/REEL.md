# Reel script: Coupled Springs and Normal Modes

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Two carts between three springs.
Caption: Two carts between three springs.

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Two equal masses on a frictionless track, joined to each other and to two walls by three identical springs.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Almost any way you start them looks like a complicated dance, but it is really just two simple motions added together: a slow in-phase mode where both masses move the same way at $\omega_+ = \sqrt{k/m}$, and a fast out-of-phase mode where they move oppositely at $\omega_- = \sqrt{3k/m}$. Press + mode or - mode to see each one alone, pure and single-frequency.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Press + mode: both masses move together as one, a single slow frequency, and the two traces sit right on top of each other.
VO: Press - mode: the masses move oppositely at a faster single frequency, and the traces become mirror images.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: push them apart and they bounce against each other faster. Every possible motion is just these two patterns added together.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
