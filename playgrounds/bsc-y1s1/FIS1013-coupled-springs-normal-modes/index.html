Coupled springs: normal modes and beats

What you are seeing: two equal masses on a frictionless track, coupled by three identical springs between two fixed walls. The classical small-oscillation problem has two normal modes: a symmetric in-phase mode at ω+=k/m\omega_+ = \sqrt{k/m}ω+​=k/m​, and an antisymmetric out-of-phase mode at ω−=3k/m\omega_- = \sqrt{3k/m}ω−​=3k/m​. Generic initial conditions excite both modes; the visible motion is the superposition.

Press the + mode or - mode button to start in a pure eigenmode.
