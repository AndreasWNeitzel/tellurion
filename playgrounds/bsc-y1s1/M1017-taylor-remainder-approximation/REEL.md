## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The degree-$n$ one, $P_n(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k! }(x-a)^k$, is built to match $f$ in value and in its first $n$ derivatives at the centre $a$, so right there the two are indistinguishable.
Caption: the physics, simply
