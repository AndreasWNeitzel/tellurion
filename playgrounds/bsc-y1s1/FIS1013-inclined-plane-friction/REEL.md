# Reel script: Inclined Plane Friction

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Tilt a ramp under a block.
Caption: Tilt a ramp under a block.

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: A block on a ramp, held by friction until the slope gets too steep.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Gravity splits into a part pressing the block into the surface and a part pulling it down the slope. Friction can match that down-slope pull only up to a ceiling, $\mu_s m g \cos\theta$.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Lower the slope below the critical angle (the dashed line on the diagnostic): the block freezes, and friction quietly grows to match the pull, no more.
VO: Raise theta just past the critical angle: the block barely breaks free, and the diagnostic shows the friction line drop from its static ceiling down to the kinetic level.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The scene shows the force vectors on the block; the diagnostic below plots the down-slope pull against the friction limit, so the crossover at $\theta_c$ and the static-to-kinetic drop are both visible.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
