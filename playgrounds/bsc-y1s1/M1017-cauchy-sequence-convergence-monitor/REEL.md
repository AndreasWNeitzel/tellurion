## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A sequence converges if its tail eventually fits inside any band you choose, however thin.
Caption: A sequence converges if its tail eventual…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: How can you tell a sequence converges without knowing what it converges to?
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Cauchy's answer: look at how tightly the terms bunch up far out. A sequence is Cauchy if, for every tolerance $\varepsilon$, there is a point $N_0$ beyond which every pair of terms is within $\varepsilon$ of each other, $|a_n - a_m| \lt \varepsilon$.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Slide the tail start N₀ outward: for a convergent sequence the width strip collapses toward zero, the terms bunch arbitrarily tightly.
VO: Demand a smaller ε: you need a larger N₀ before the tail fits inside it, the response to the challenge that defines Cauchy.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The harmonic sum is the cautionary case: its terms shrink, yet the tail width never falls below about $\ln 10$, so it is not Cauchy and does not converge.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
