Catenary: shape of a hanging chain

What you are seeing: a uniform, perfectly flexible chain suspended between two fixed pegs at $(\pm 1, 0)$. The shape is $y(x) = a \cosh(x / a) - a$, where $a = T_0 / (\mu g)$ is the catenary parameter (horizontal tension $T_0$, linear mass density $\mu$, gravity $g$). As $a$ grows the chain is pulled taut and the cosh flattens toward a parabola $y \approx x^2 / 2a$; as $a$ shrinks the chain sags more.

The orange dashed line shows the parabolic approximation $y = x^2 / 2a$ for the same $a$. For small sag the two curves agree; for large sag (small $a$) the parabola underestimates the chain shape because cosh grows faster than its quadratic Taylor expansion.
