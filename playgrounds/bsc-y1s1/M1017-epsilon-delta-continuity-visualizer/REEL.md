## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: The rigorous definition of a limit, drawn as a box.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Continuity at a point $x_0$ says: for every tolerance $\varepsilon$ you demand on the output, there is a tolerance $\delta$ on the input that delivers it, $|x - x_0| \lt \delta \Rightarrow |f(x) - f(x_0)| \lt \varepsilon$. Pick $\varepsilon$ and watch the widest $\delta$ that works appear: the curve over the $\delta$-interval stays trapped inside the $\varepsilon$-band, the graph leaving the box only through its sides, never its top or bottom.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Shrink epsilon: the box narrows and the widest delta shrinks with it, but stays positive. The moving point never leaves the box.
VO: Switch to the jump and set x₀ = 0: for small epsilon no delta works, the box cannot be drawn, and δ(ε) reads zero. That is a discontinuity.
Caption: your turn
