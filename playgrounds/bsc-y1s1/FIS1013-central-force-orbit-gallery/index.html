Central-Force Orbit Gallery

A particle in a central potential $V(r)=k\,r^{p}$. The orbit (left) is integrated with symplectic velocity-Verlet; the effective potential $V_{\text{eff}}(r)=V(r)+L^2/2\mu r^2$ (right) sets the radial turning points and the orbit class. Only the inverse-square ($p=-1$) and harmonic ($p=2$) laws give closed orbits (Bertrand); everything else is a precessing rosette.
