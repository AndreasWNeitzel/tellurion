# Reel script: Big-O Empirical Scaling

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Watch a real sort run one comparison at a time and count them; the measured totals land on the N^2 and N log N curves.
Caption: Watch a real sort run one comparison at a…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Big-O is usually a formula on a whiteboard.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Here it is a thing you can watch happen. The scene runs a real sort on a shuffled array, one comparison at a time, and counts every comparison it makes.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Watch a sort run and the comparison counter climb; when it finishes, the array reshuffles and runs again.
VO: Switch between bubble, insertion, and merge at the same N and speed: every step takes the same time, so the clock at the top shows the quadratic sorts taking far longer to finish than merge.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: the measured totals land on the N^2 and N log N curves.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
