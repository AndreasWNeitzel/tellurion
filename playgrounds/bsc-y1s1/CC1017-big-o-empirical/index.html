Big-O empirical scaling

What you are seeing: the operation counts of four canonical complexity classes plotted on a log-log axis: $N$ (linear), $N \log_2 N$ (typical comparison-sort), $N^2$ (naive nested loop), $N^3$ (matrix multiplication or naive matrix inversion). At $N = 10^4$ the naive quadratic is already $10^8$ ops, the cubic is $10^{12}$; at modern CPU speeds these are tractable seconds vs days.
