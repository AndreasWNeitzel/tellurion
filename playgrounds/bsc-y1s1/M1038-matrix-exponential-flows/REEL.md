## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: The linear system $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ has one clean solution, $\mathbf{x}(t) = e^{At}\mathbf{x}_0$, and the matrix exponential is the operator that slides every starting point forward in time at once, the flow of the system.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: What the flow looks like is decided entirely by the eigenvalues of $A$. Two real eigenvalues of the same sign make a node, points racing straight out from the origin or straight in; opposite signs make a saddle, drawn in along one eigendirection and flung out along the other; a complex pair makes a spiral, winding in if the real part is negative and out if it is positive; and a purely imaginary pair makes a centre, closed loops that neither grow nor decay.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Dial the four matrix entries a, b, c, d (or pick a preset) and watch the violet unit circle deform under $e^{At}$: it stretches into an ellipse for a node, shears hyperbolically for a saddle, rotates for a centre, and spirals for a spiral.
VO: Drag the green initial point: the white dot rides its exact $e^{At}$ trajectory, spiralling in, racing out, or looping depending on the eigenvalues.
Caption: your turn
