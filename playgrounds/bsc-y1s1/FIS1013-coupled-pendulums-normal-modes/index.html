Coupled pendulums and normal modes

What you are seeing: two identical pendulums of length LLL and mass mmm, coupled by a spring of constant kkk attached at distance ddd from the pivot. The small-angle EOM has two normal modes: symmetric (both swing in phase) at ω+=g/L\omega_+ = \sqrt{g/L}ω+​=g/L​, and antisymmetric (opposite phase) at ω−=g/L+2kd2/(mL2)\omega_- = \sqrt{g/L + 2 k d^2 / (m L^2)}ω−​=g/L+2kd2/(mL2)​.
