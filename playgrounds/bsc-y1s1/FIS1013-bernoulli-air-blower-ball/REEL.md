# Reel script: Bernoulli Air-Blower Ball

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A light ball hangs in a tilted air jet and self-centres: tip the nozzle and the ball leans with it, weaken the blower and it drops.
Caption: A light ball hangs in a tilted air jet an…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: The hair-dryer trick: a light ball hangs in a jet of air and refuses to fall out, even when you tilt the stream sideways.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Two things hold it. Vertically, the jet's drag pushes up; the jet slows with height, so there is exactly one height where drag equals the ball's weight, and it is stable, drift up and drag weakens so the ball sinks back.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Tilt the nozzle: the ball leans with the jet instead of falling out, the Bernoulli restoring force keeping it on the axis.
VO: Lower the blower power: the drag curve drops, the balance height falls, and below a threshold the curve no longer reaches the weight line, so the ball drops.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The scene shows the jet, colored by speed, cradling the ball; the diagnostic plots the upward drag against height and marks where it balances gravity.
