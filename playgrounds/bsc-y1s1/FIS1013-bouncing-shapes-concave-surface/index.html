Bouncing balls in a concave bowl

What you are seeing: balls falling under gravity into a bowl whose profile you choose. On each contact the velocity is reflected about the local tangent and the normal component is scaled by the restitution $e$. The bowl shape sets the motion: a parabola gives near-simple-harmonic swings, the V-bowl piecewise constant acceleration, the quartic a strongly amplitude-dependent period, the arc a pendulum. With $e = 1$ the balls bounce forever.
