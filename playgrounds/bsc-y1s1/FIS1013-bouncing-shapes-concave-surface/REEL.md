# Reel script: Bouncing Shapes Concave Surface

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Drop balls into a bowl you choose: a parabola swings them like a spring, a V-bowl ticks, a quartic drifts, an arc swings like a pendulum.
Caption: Drop balls into a bowl you choose: a para…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: Build a picture out of thousands of little balls, hold it in the air, and let go.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Gravity does not care what the picture was: every ball falls into the bowl and bounces. On each bounce the velocity reflects about the local surface tangent, the normal part flipped and scaled by the restitution $e$, so with $e = 1$ the balls bounce forever and energy is conserved, while $e \lt 1$ bleeds energy away on every hit until the shape pools into a still layer at the bottom.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Pick a shape and drop it: it holds for an instant, then shatters into the bowl and pools at the bottom.
VO: Set restitution e = 1: nothing is lost, the balls bounce forever and the energy line stays flat.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The diagnostic tracks the total energy, flat when nothing is lost and a descending staircase when it is.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
