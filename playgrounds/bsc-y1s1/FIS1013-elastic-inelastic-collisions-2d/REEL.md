# Reel script: Elastic and Inelastic Collisions in 2D

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Two pucks glance off each other.
Caption: Two pucks glance off each other.

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: What you are seeing: two disks of mass m1,m2m_1, m_2m1​,m2​ collide obliquely with an adjustable impact parameter bbb and coefficient of restitution eee.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The collision is resolved along the contact normal: that component reverses scaled by eee, the tangential component is unchanged. Momentum is conserved componentwise for any eee; kinetic energy only for e=1e = 1e=1.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Set $e = 1$ for a perfect bounce: the kinetic-energy line stays flat. Lower $e$ and watch energy drop at the moment of impact while momentum does not.
VO: Slide the impact parameter $b$ from a head-on hit to a glancing graze and watch the scatter angle open up.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: kinetic energy survives only if the collision is perfectly elastic. Dial the bounciness and the impact offset and watch both bookkeeping rules at once.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
