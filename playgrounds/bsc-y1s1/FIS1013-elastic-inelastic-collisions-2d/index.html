Elastic and inelastic 2D collisions

What you are seeing: two disks of mass $m_1, m_2$ collide obliquely with an adjustable impact parameter $b$ and coefficient of restitution $e$. The collision is resolved along the contact normal: that component reverses scaled by $e$, the tangential component is unchanged. Momentum is conserved componentwise for any $e$; kinetic energy only for $e = 1$. Head-on ($b = 0$) is the familiar 1D case.
