Elastic and inelastic 1D collisions

What you are seeing: two balls of mass $m_1, m_2$ approach with velocities $u_1, u_2$. After collision (restitution $e$) the post-collision velocities follow the closed-form formula $v_1, v_2 = f(m_1, m_2, u_1, u_2, e)$. Momentum is conserved for any $e$; kinetic energy only for $e = 1$. Source: Marion-Thornton Ch. 9 (`marion-thornton`).
