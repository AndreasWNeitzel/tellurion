Elastic and Inelastic Collisions in 2D

What you are seeing: two disks of mass m1,m2m_1, m_2m1​,m2​ collide obliquely with an adjustable impact parameter bbb and coefficient of restitution eee. The collision is resolved along the contact normal: that component reverses scaled by eee, the tangential component is unchanged. Momentum is conserved componentwise for any eee; kinetic energy only for e=1e = 1e=1. Head-on (b=0b = 0b=0) is the familiar 1D case.

WHAT TO TRY

  • Set $e = 1$ for a perfect bounce: the kinetic-energy line stays flat. Lower $e$ and watch energy drop at the moment of impact while momentum does not.
  • Slide the impact parameter $b$ from a head-on hit to a glancing graze and watch the scatter angle open up.
  • Change the masses: the total-momentum arrow in the corner never changes length, however the two disks share it out.
