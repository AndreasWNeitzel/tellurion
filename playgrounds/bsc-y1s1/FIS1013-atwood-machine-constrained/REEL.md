## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The same gravity pulls on both masses, yet it is the rope tension that keeps the lighter one from falling freely. Vary the mass ratio and the acceleration runs from zero (balanced masses) toward g (one mass dominating).
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Set m₁ = m₂: the system freezes at zero acceleration, and the two tensions stay equal even with a heavy pulley (no spin-up means no net torque).
VO: Raise m₁ with m₂ fixed: the acceleration climbs toward g, and on the diagnostic both tension curves rise while T₁ pulls ahead of T₂.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: The panel beside the rig plugs the live numbers into a = (m₁ − m₂) g / (m₁ + m₂ + I/R²), and the diagnostic below tracks the acceleration and both tensions against the mass ratio.
