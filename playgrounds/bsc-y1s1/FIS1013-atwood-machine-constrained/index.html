Atwood Machine with a Massive Pulley

Two masses on an inextensible rope over a pulley of finite moment of inertia. The rope constraint gives $a=(m_1-m_2)g/(m_1+m_2+I/R^2)$, and the pulley inertia makes the two tensions unequal: $T_1=m_1(g-a)$, $T_2=m_2(g+a)$. Toggle the pulley between a uniform disk ($I=\tfrac12 MR^2$) and a thin ring ($I=MR^2$), or set its mass to zero for the classical result.
