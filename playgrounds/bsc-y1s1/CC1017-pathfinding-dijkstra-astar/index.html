Dijkstra vs A* on a city grid

What you are seeing: the same shortest-path problem on a procedural city (streets cost 1, piazzas cost 4, buildings and the river are walls). Left, Dijkstra floods outward in all directions; right, A* uses a goal-direction heuristic and drives a tight beam toward the target. Both settle on the same optimal route, but A* explores far fewer cells, the count is live.
