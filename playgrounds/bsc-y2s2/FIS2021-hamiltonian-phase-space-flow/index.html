Hamiltonian phase-space flow

What you are seeing: the orbits of Hamilton's equations in the $(q, p)$ plane. Click anywhere to release a tracer at $(q_0, p_0)$; the integrator steps in time and traces an orbit at constant energy $H(q, p)$. For the pendulum, you can see librations (closed loops) below the separatrix $E = 1$ and rotations (open curves) above. Source: Lemos Analytical Mechanics Ch. 6 (`lemos-mech`).
