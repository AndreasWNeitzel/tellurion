Dipole Radiation in 3D

An oscillating electric dipole radiates with the angular pattern $\sin^2\theta$: nothing along its axis, maximum in the equatorial plane, the familiar toroidal donut. The rotating 3D surface is that pattern; the pulsing charges are the source and the rings are outgoing wavefronts. The time-averaged power is the Larmor form $P = \mu_0 p_0^2 \omega^4 / (12\pi c)$, scaling as $\omega^4$, and in the far zone $\mathbf E$, $\mathbf B$ and $\hat{\mathbf r}$ are mutually orthogonal with $|\mathbf E| = c|\mathbf B|$. Switch to a magnetic dipole or a half-wave antenna to compare the lobes and directivity.
