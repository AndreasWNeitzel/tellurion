## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Treating the radiation in a hot cavity as a sum of wave modes, each carrying the same thermal energy $k_B T$, the Rayleigh-Jeans law says the spectral radiance is $2c k_B T/\lambda^4$, which grows without bound as the wavelength shrinks: a hot oven should blaze with infinite ultraviolet light. It does not, and the disagreement was called the ultraviolet catastrophe.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Raise the temperature: the Planck peak slides to shorter wavelengths (a cool star is red, a hot one blue) and the whole curve grows, while the Rayleigh-Jeans curve always shoots off the top.
VO: Notice that the two curves agree at long wavelengths and only part ways toward the short, ultraviolet end, where the classical law diverges and Planck's turns over.
