# Reel script: Mandelbrot Rainbow Explorer

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype. No em-dashes, no emoji.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: One equation, z -> z 2 + c, iterated for every point of the plane, draws the most intricate object in mathematics, infinitely detailed at every zoom.
Caption: One equation, z -> z 2 + c, iterated for…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: The Mandelbrot set is the set of complex numbers c for which the iteration z -> z 2 + c, started at z = 0, stays bounded forever.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: The playground colours every pixel (a value of c) by how fast its orbit escapes the disc |z| <= 2; points that never escape form the black body of the set. Its boundary is a fractal of unlimited complexity, with miniature copies of the whole set buried at every scale, so zooming in never simplifies it.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Click anywhere to recenter and let it auto-zoom: the boundary of the set is infinitely detailed, and every dive reveals miniature copies of the whole Mandelbrot shape.
VO: Jump to a named landmark (Seahorse Valley, a satellite mini-set, the elephant valley): each is a different self-similar structure on the fractal boundary.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: This single quadratic recurrence is the canonical demonstration that trivial rules can generate infinite structure.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
