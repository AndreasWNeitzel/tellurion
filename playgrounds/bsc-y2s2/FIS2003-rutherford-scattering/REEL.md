# Reel script: Rutherford Scattering

Vertical 9:16, about 30 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the playground in portrait (820x1040), voiceover plus on-screen captions. Voice: first person, direct, no hype.

## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: A few alpha particles bounced straight back off a gold foil, and that was enough to put a tiny nucleus at the heart of the atom.
Caption: A few alpha particles bounced straight ba…

## Beat 2, the reveal (3 to 10s)
VO: In 1909 a few alpha particles fired at a thin gold foil bounced almost straight back, which Rutherford called as incredible as a shell rebounding off tissue paper.
Caption: what you are seeing

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: A diffuse cloud of charge could never do that; only a tiny, dense, charged nucleus could turn a fast alpha around. Each alpha follows a hyperbola in the nucleus's Coulomb field, and how sharply it bends is fixed by its impact parameter, the sideways miss-distance of its incoming line.
Caption: the physics, simply

## Beat 4, try it (22 to 33s)
VO: Slide the impact parameter toward zero: the highlighted orbit whips around the nucleus and back-scatters, the angle climbing toward 180 degrees.
VO: Raise the nuclear charge or lower the alpha energy: the closest-approach circle D grows and every orbit bends harder.
Caption: your turn

## Beat 5, payoff and CTA (33 to 40s)
VO: Slide the energy and nuclear charge to squeeze or stretch the orbits, and pick an impact parameter to read its angle and closest approach.
VO: Full interactive version at tellurion.dev. Follow for one of these a day.
Caption: tellurion.dev
