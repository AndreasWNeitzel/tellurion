Lorenz attractor

What you are seeing: a simplified model of atmospheric convection, due to Edward Lorenz in 1963. The trajectory in (x,y,z)(x, y, z)(x,y,z) never settles down and never repeats, but it stays trapped in a butterfly-shaped region of space. This was the first rigorously studied example of deterministic chaos: noise-free, completely deterministic, yet long-term prediction is impossible.
