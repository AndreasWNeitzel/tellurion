## Beat 1, hook (0 to 3s)
VO: Three ways to hunt a root: bisection never fails but crawls, Newton races when it has the derivative, the secant splits the difference.

## Beat 3, the mechanism (10 to 22s)
VO: Bisection only needs a sign change and halves the bracket every step, guaranteed but linear. Newton-Raphson uses the derivative and converges quadratically near a root, but can diverge from a bad start.
Caption: the physics, simply
