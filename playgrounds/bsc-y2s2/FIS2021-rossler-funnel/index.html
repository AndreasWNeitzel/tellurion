Rossler funnel attractor

What you are seeing: a cousin of the Lorenz attractor, written down by Otto Rossler in 1976 as the simplest 3D system that still produces chaos (only one nonlinear term, z⋅xz \cdot xz⋅x). The trajectory winds outward on a flat spiral, then a single fold throws it back to the centre. Push the control parameter ccc past about 5.75.75.7 and the spiral grows a strong vertical lobe; the geometry earns its "funnel" name.
