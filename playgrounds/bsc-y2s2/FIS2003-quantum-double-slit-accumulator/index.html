Quantum Double Slit, One Particle at a Time

Particles arrive at the screen one by one, each a single dot whose position is drawn by the Born rule from $|\psi|^2$. Individually random, together they build the interference fringes of spacing $\Delta y = \lambda L / d$ under the single-slit envelope. Turn up the which-path detector and the fringes wash out: knowing which slit each particle took destroys the interference, leaving only the envelope. The side panel tracks the running histogram against the analytic $|\psi|^2$ with the live fringe visibility.
