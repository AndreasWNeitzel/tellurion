Radioactive Decay Chain

A heavy nucleus walks a decay series to stable lead. Each step is a real transmutation: alpha decay sheds a He-4 cluster ($Z{-}2$, $N{-}2$), beta-minus turns a neutron into a proton ($Z{+}1$, $N{-}1$). The Q value comes from the semi-empirical mass formula and the alpha half-life from the Geiger-Nuttall law $\log_{10} t_{1/2} = aZ_d/\sqrt{Q} - b$. The side panel is the Segre chart tracing the path from the parent to the stable endpoint (Pb-206 for U-238, Pb-208 for Th-232).
