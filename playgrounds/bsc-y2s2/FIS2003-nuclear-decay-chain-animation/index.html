Radioactive Decay Chain

A heavy nucleus (rendered in 3D as close-packed protons and neutrons) walks a decay series to stable lead. Each step is a real transmutation: alpha decay sheds a He-4 cluster ($Z{-}2$, $N{-}2$); beta-minus turns a neutron into a proton ($Z{+}1$, $N{-}1$). The Q value comes from the semi-empirical mass formula and the alpha half-life from the Geiger-Nuttall law $\log_{10} t_{1/2} = 1.72 \, Z_d / \sqrt{Q} - 53.6$, with the slope set by Gamow tunnelling through the Coulomb barrier. The three panels: the nucleus (left), the Geiger-Nuttall log half-life trend (top right), the Segre chart path to the stable endpoint (bottom right).
