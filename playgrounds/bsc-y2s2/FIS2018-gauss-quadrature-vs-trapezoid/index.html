Gauss-Legendre vs trapezoid quadrature

What you are seeing: two ways of approximating ∫−11f(x) dx\int_{-1}^{1} f(x)\, dx∫−11​f(x)dx. The trapezoidal rule uses n+1n + 1n+1 equispaced points and converges as O(h2)O(h^2)O(h2) for smooth fff. Gauss-Legendre uses nnn optimized nodes (roots of PnP_nPn​) with matching weights, exact for polynomials up to degree 2n−12n - 12n−1. For analytic fff the GL error decays exponentially.
