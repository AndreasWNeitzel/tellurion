Liouvillian flow on the pendulum phase space

Drop a Gaussian blob of 256 tracers anywhere in the pendulum phase space (theta, p). Each tracer evolves under the Hamiltonian H = p2/2 − cos(theta) via velocity-Verlet. The cloud rotates, shears, and filaments, but its phase-space area is preserved (Liouville's theorem). The dashed curve is the separatrix p = ±2 cos(theta/2): below it orbits librate, above it they rotate. Drag the dashed circle (handle) to set the blob center.
