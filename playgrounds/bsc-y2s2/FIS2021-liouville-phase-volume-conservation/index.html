Liouville's theorem: phase-space volume is conserved

What you are seeing: a small rectangular patch of initial conditions in $(q, p)$ is evolved under the pendulum flow. The patch stretches and twists, but its phase-space area stays constant. This is Liouville's theorem, a consequence of the symplectic structure of Hamiltonian flow. Source: Lemos Ch. 6 (`lemos-mech`).
