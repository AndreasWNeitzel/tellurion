Harmonic oscillator coherent state

A coherent state of the 1D quantum harmonic oscillator |alpha> is the eigenvector of the lowering operator a. Its probability density |psi(x, t)|^2 is a Gaussian of fixed width 1/sqrt 2 whose mean follows the classical orbit x_0(t) = sqrt 2 Re(alpha e^{-i omega t}). The blue curve is |psi|^2; the cat-2 trace is Re(psi); the parabolic background is the harmonic potential V(x) = x^2 / 2. Watch the wave packet oscillate without spreading, the hallmark of coherent states.
