Harmonic oscillator coherent state

What you are seeing: a quantum wave packet that slides back and forth inside a parabolic bowl without spreading out. Classical balls in this bowl oscillate forever; a typical quantum wave packet would smear and lose coherence. The "coherent state" $|\alpha\rangle$ is the special wave packet that keeps its Gaussian shape, with its centre tracing the classical orbit $x_0(t) = \sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{Re}(\alpha e^{-i \omega t})$.

The blue curve is the probability density $|\psi(x, t)|^2$; the orange wiggle underneath is $\mathrm{Re}(\psi)$; the dashed parabola is the potential $V(x) = x^2 / 2$. The red dot is the classical particle that would correspond to the same orbit. They move together. The expected number of energy quanta is $\langle n \rangle = |\alpha|^2$.
