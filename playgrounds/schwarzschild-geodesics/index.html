Schwarzschild geodesics in the equatorial plane

A massive test particle on a Schwarzschild background, geometric units G = c = M = 1. The radial motion is a 1D Hamiltonian in the effective potential Veff(r) = −1/r + L2/(2r2) − L2/r3; the last term is the relativistic correction that causes perihelion precession. Velocity-Verlet from shared/js/engine/symplectic.js integrates the radial Hamiltonian; the angular coordinate ϕ advances as L/r2. The dashed inner circle is the event horizon r = 2; the dashed outer circle is the ISCO r = 6.
